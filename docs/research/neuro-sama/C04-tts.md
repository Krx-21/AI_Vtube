# C04-tts — TTS main voice
Verdict counts: {'disputed': 7, 'likely': 5, 'unverifiable': 2, 'refuted': 1, 'confirmed': 2, 'meta': 1}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
What is genuinely established about Neuro's voice stack is mostly what Vedal has said about the voice he has NOT shipped. At T1 (his own 10 June 2025 dev stream) and T2 (his official vedal.ai New Year Resolutions 2026 page), "V3 voice" is a speaking-TTS upgrade he has chased since about 2023, pursued along two tracks — pre-training TTS models from scratch starting around November/December 2024 with "decent results" by March 2025, plus roughly 100 manual fine-tuning runs on open- and closed-source models "with a bunch of different companies," including genetic-algorithm search over speaker embeddings — and as of mid-2025 he judged it "passable" but "not a big enough upgrade," while the 2026 resolutions page still lists "Create V3 voice????????????????????" as unmet. The hard constraint he states is identity preservation, not quality: "it's super easy to give Neuro a voice upgrade... what's super hard to do is to keep the same voice and have an upgrade," and he concedes it is "impossible to compete with the multi-million dollar training run text-to-speech models" — which is the strongest indirect support for the long-standing belief that the shipped main voice is a commercial third-party TTS. By contrast, the specific engine claim (Microsoft Azure voice "Ashley," pitched up 25%) is T5 only: it appears as uncited fan-wiki text on neurosama.fandom and virtualyoutuber.fandom, is absent from English Wikipedia entirely (verified 22 Sep 2026 in both rendered text and wikitext), and its main "independent" corroboration (the lin-guanguo research page) is an uncited copy of the same sentence that self-flags its neighbouring figures as unverified — so it should be reported as undated community lore, never verified for 2025–2026 and never attributed to Vedal. Neuro's STT engine is unknown at every tier: the official VedalAI neuro-sdk documents only a text-in/text-out game API with no audio component at all, Wikipedia never mentions speech-to-text, and every "Whisper" attribution traces to clone projects (kimjammer/Neuro's faster-whisper tiny.en), which must be excluded. Singing is described by three separate wiki pages as a subsystem outside the LLM that shares one audio output with chat-TTS — corroborated indirectly by Vedal listing "duets in karaoke" as its own outstanding item in the April 2026 rewrite — but its V1/V2/V3 labels, its interruption bug, and above all its engine are unsourced; the sole engine name ever floated, "Synthesizer V Studio Pro 2" on NamuWiki's song subpage, is a machine-translated line whose own dating is self-contradictory and is absent from the Korean original, so no singing engine is established. Latency, finally, is the one voice-adjacent quantity with dated numbers: +400 ms accepted as the price of an intelligence upgrade (Vedal, April 2024, T1), and 900 ms–1 s reduced to "mostly 700 ms or lower" for both Neuro and Evil (22 July 2024, wiki-relayed dev stream, T5); nothing dated after the April 2026 rewrite or the May 2026 server rebuild updates either figure, so all engine- and latency-level statements in this cluster should be presented as pre-rewrite.

## Summary B (skeptic)
Almost nothing about Neuro's voice stack is established above fan-wiki tier, and the one piece of hard evidence in this cluster overturns the finders' own framing. At T1, Vedal's 10 June 2025 dev stream establishes that "V3 voice" is a genuine spoken-TTS project, not an avatar label: he pre-trained TTS models from scratch starting around November/December 2024 (usable results by ~March 2025), experimented with genetic algorithms over speaker embeddings, then performed roughly 100 manual fine-tuning runs across open- and closed-source models with partner companies — and still declined to ship it, calling it "honestly passable" but "not a big enough upgrade," with the hard constraint being to improve naturalness while keeping the same recognisable voice. No source found confirms V3 voice shipped by September 2026, so the current main voice is formally unknown. The widely repeated "Microsoft Azure TTS, voice 'Ashley', pitched up 25%" claim collapses on inspection: English Wikipedia (re-read 2026-09-22) names no vendor, voice, pitch or engine at all, and the apparently independent corroborations — the Neuro-sama Fandom "nutshell" section, the Virtual YouTuber wiki, and the lin-guanguo research page — are uncited and mutually downstream, giving one undated origin rather than four witnesses, with nothing tying it to 2025-2026. STT is worse: no source of any tier names an engine, the official neuro-sdk documentation contains no mention of audio whatsoever, and every appearance of Whisper/faster-whisper traces to the kimjammer/Neuro clone, as does the "TTS into VTube Studio over a virtual audio cable" description, which is additionally contradicted by Vedal's own account of a custom Unity renderer his core process survives independently of. Singing is the best-supported structural fact — multiple wikis agree it is a separate system from the chat/LLM path (first song "Blinding Lights", 4 Jan 2023), and Vedal indirectly corroborates this by listing karaoke duets as a distinct unimplemented work item in the April 2026 rewrite — but its V1/V2/V3 history is uncited, the wikis contradict each other on whether speech interrupts singing or singing pauses speech, and the only named singing engine anywhere ("Synthesizer V Studio Pro 2", one machine-translated NamuWiki subpage) is uncorroborated and absent from both NamuWiki main articles. What is solid at news tier is the production side rather than the technology: official 2026 releases use "synthetically produced vocals" with human songwriting, production and vocal production by QueenPB, feeding November 2026 EPs and a 19 December 2026 live concert. Open for a report: the current TTS engine, the STT engine, the singing synthesis engine, whether V3 voice ever shipped, and whether the March-June 2026 rewrite (which Vedal said targets "lower latency") altered any of them.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction 1 (turn latency 700 ms vs 1-3 s vs 'low latency'): resolved as three different things measured at three different times, none of them current. Vedal-primary gives only deltas and qualitative statements (+400 ms as the price of an intelligence upgrade, April 2024); the '900ms-1s -> mostly 700ms or lower, also applicable to Evil Neuro' figure is a wiki recap of the 22 July 2024 dev stream (T5, dated, historical); the '1-3 seconds' figure is an observer's impression of full turn time including LLM generation and audio playback; Wikipedia's 'low latency' is an unquantified journalist line (Faulkner, GameSpot, 8 Dec 2025). The report should present only the 2024-dated numbers, labelled as pre-rewrite, and note Vedal's March 2026 statement that the rewrite brings 'lower latency' with no number published since.
- Contradiction 6 ('V3' label collision): resolved decisively with primary evidence. There are at least three non-commensurable V3s. (a) V3 avatar = 19 December 2024 Live2D model, designed by Anny, rigged by Kitanya (Wikipedia, T4). (b) V3 singing voice = a fan-wiki label for the third overhaul of the karaoke subsystem, which NamuWiki dates to June 2023 (T5, uncited). (c) V3 voice = Vedal's speaking-TTS upgrade project, unshipped as of his 10 June 2025 dev stream and still listed as an unmet resolution on vedal.ai for 2026 (T1/T2). Therefore no spoken-TTS engine change accompanied the December 2024 'V3 Model' release, and the 'V3 voice model shipped mid-2025' reading is wrong. A fourth 'V3' — fan RVC voice-clone models sold as 'Neuro-sama V3' — is unrelated to all of the above.
- Contradiction 7 (TTS engine confidence, Azure 'Ashley' +25%): resolved in favour of the stricter grading. The claim exists only as uncited present-tense fan-wiki text (neurosama.fandom 'nutshell' section and virtualyoutuber.fandom 'Trivia > System'), with no date and no footnote, on pages whose other statements are footnoted. English Wikipedia — the better-curated wiki — declines to name any engine (verified 22 Sep 2026 in rendered text and wikitext: neither 'Azure' nor 'Ashley' occurs). The apparently independent lin-guanguo research page is an uncited copy of the same sentence and self-flags neighbouring figures as UNVERIFIED, so it is not corroboration. Grade T5/unverified, present it as undated community lore, and note that Vedal's own 'impossible to compete with multi-million dollar TTS training runs' remark makes a commercial third-party TTS plausible without naming one.
- Contradiction 8 (next-gen TTS fate — scrapped for Evil vs a 'V3 voice' shipping mid-2025): resolved as a false dilemma built on a mislabel. The 'scrapped, repurposed for Evil' story is undated 2023-era uncited wiki text and is itself contradicted by neurosama.fandom's account that Evil received Neuro's *discarded V2 voice*; meanwhile nothing shipped in mid-2025 — Vedal's 10 June 2025 dev stream says the V3 voice was 'not a big enough upgrade' and vedal.ai still lists creating it as a 2026 resolution. Conclusion for the report: Neuro's main speaking voice has been effectively frozen while Vedal iterates on a replacement he has repeatedly refused to ship, and the 2025 dev-stream titles referencing 'V3 Voice' describe progress reports, not a release.
- Clone flag 4 (Whisper as Neuro's STT): upheld and strengthened. Whisper/faster-whisper appears only in kimjammer/Neuro and a dev.to tutorial copying it. Independently, the official VedalAI neuro-sdk documentation contains no audio/STT component at all and English Wikipedia never mentions speech-to-text, so there is no competing real-Neuro source to weigh against the clones. Any statement that Neuro 'uses Whisper' is pure clone contamination; the only defensible statement is that she has used unnamed 'speech recognition software' for collab audio since February 2023 and that Vedal later improved it to distinguish sound effects from speech.
- Clone flag 5 (RVC / so-vits-svc for singing): upheld. Every RVC/so-vits hit is a fan-made cover-generator voice model trained on Neuro's broadcast audio (fakeyou/storyteller.ai, jammable, voicedub, vocalize.fm, voice-models.com, 101soundboards), and Vedal's own 2026 resolutions page shows the real 'V3 voice' was still unbuilt, so a product marketed as 'Neuro-sama V3' cannot be his. Combined with the collapse of the Synthesizer V attribution (self-inconsistent dating, absent from the NamuWiki Korean original), the honest conclusion is that Vedal's singing engine is unidentified at every tier — this should be stated as an open gap, not filled with any of these names.
- Contradiction 1 (turn latency, ~700ms vs 1-3s vs 'low latency'): partially resolved. These are different measurement points at different dates, not competing measurements of one thing. The only quantitative figure is a wiki recap of the 22 Jul 2024 dev stream (900ms-1s down to ~700ms, for both Neuro and Evil). Wikipedia's 'low latency' is qualitative and, critically, attributed to Neuro as a whole, not to TTS. Vedal's own T1 statements show the budget is dominated by non-TTS stages: +400 ms accepted in Apr 2024 for an intelligence upgrade, and vision deliberately 'stunted ... for the sake of latency'. The report should present one dated historical figure (~700 ms, Jul 2024) and state that no post-rewrite 2026 figure exists, rather than reconciling the three into a single number.
- Contradiction 6 ('V3' label collision): resolved by T1 evidence, and it resolves against the finders. There are at least three distinct V3s and they are not commensurable: the Live2D avatar released 19 Dec 2024 (Wikipedia: 'rigged by Kitanya and designed by Anny'); the singing plugin's third generation (uncited Fandom, dated June 2023 by NamuWiki); and 'V3 voice', which Vedal himself names on his 10 Jun 2025 dev stream as a spoken-TTS upgrade project. Claim r2-04#04's assertion that V3 is only an avatar label with no confirmed TTS meaning is therefore refuted. The unverified 'V3 model, 25 March 2025' brain upgrade remains a fourth, unsourced label and should be dropped.
- Contradiction 7 (TTS engine confidence, Azure 'Ashley' +25%): resolved in favour of angle 01's sceptical grade, and downgraded further. English Wikipedia (re-fetched 2026-09-22) does not mention Azure or Ashley at all. The apparent corroborations are not independent: the Neuro-sama Fandom 'nutshell' section is uncited, the Virtual YouTuber wiki repeats it, and the lin-guanguo page repeats it with no citation while itself warning that adjacent technical figures trace back to Fandom wikis only. One undated origin, restated three times. The claim must be reported as unverified fan-wiki lore with no date attached, never as a medium-confidence fact, and explicitly not as a 2025-2026 statement.
- Contradiction 8 (fate of the next-gen TTS): resolved, and both halves of the old story are weaker than reported. The 'trialled for Neuro, scrapped for latency and backlash, given to Evil' account is a single undated fan-wiki sentence whose citation I could not verify (Fandom blocked, HTTP 402), sitting next to a different account on the same wiki in which Evil inherited Neuro's 'discarded V2 voice'; at best it is a 2023-era episode. The 'V3 voice model shipped mid-2025' half is contradicted by T1: on 10 Jun 2025 Vedal said the V3 voice was 'not a big enough upgrade' and did not ship it. The honest report line is that Neuro's main spoken voice has no confirmed upgrade on record, and that the most recent T1 datapoint is an unshipped upgrade attempt.
- Contradiction 11 (Twitch subscriber figures): resolved as one measurement plus one different metric. Multiple independent outlets report the same TwitchTracker-derived ~162,459 active paid subscribers as of 2 Jan 2026 on twitch.tv/vedal987, consistent with the 'over 160,000' rounding; the 343,215 figure is a separate metric (total subscribers at the 9 Jan 2026 subathon peak, third-highest of all time). The undated ~Sep 2026 '165,268' figure from a financial X post is uncorroborated and should not be presented as a separate later measurement. None of these figures belongs in the voice-pipeline section.
- Clone flag 1 (VTube Studio + virtual audio cable): resolved as clone contamination and additionally refuted by T1. The description traces to kimjammer/Neuro and TheDyingYAK/Neuro-sama, and Vedal's own March 2026 dev stream describes a custom Unity renderer that the Neuro/Evil core 'survives outside of' and which 'can be restarted perfectly fine'. Drop it; state that the real lip-sync/animation driver is undocumented.
- Clone flag 4 (Whisper as Neuro's STT): resolved. Re-checked in Sept 2026 — Wikipedia contains no 'Whisper', the official neuro-sdk docs mention no audio at all, and no source of any tier names an STT engine for Neuro. Whisper/faster-whisper appear only in kimjammer/Neuro and a dev.to clone tutorial. Report the STT engine as unknown, and name Whisper only as an example of what clones use.
- Clone flag 5 (RVC / so-vits-svc for singing): resolved. Every RVC/so-vits 'Neuro-sama' artifact found is a third-party cover generator trained on her published audio (FakeYou/Storyteller, Jammable, VoiceDub, Vocalize, Singify, voice-models.com, fish.audio).
