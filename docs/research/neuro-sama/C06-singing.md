# C06-singing — Singing / music pipeline
Verdict counts: {'disputed': 5, 'likely': 6, 'unverifiable': 1, 'meta': 2, 'confirmed': 1}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
What is solidly established about Neuro's singing is architectural, not technological. Three mutually independent wikis agree that singing runs on a subsystem separate from the chat/LLM path — it "does not use the LLM model", the speaking TTS is paused while it plays, and the two paths could historically collide (Neuro thanking donors mid-song) — and Vedal himself corroborates the modularity at T1 by listing "duets in karaoke need to be done" as an unfinished item of the April 2026 codebase rewrite, i.e. karaoke is a discrete component that had to be re-implemented. No source at any tier names the singing engine: not Vedal, not VedalAI's repos, not Wikipedia, not either NamuWiki language edition, and the widely circulated "Synthesizer V" attribution could not be traced to any source in this pass (the "V1/V2/V3 singing plugin" nomenclature comes from a completely uncited Fandom page and should be reported as community lore, while NamuWiki separately dates a "V3 singing voice" to June 2023 — which is not the V3 Live2D avatar of December 2024 and not Vedal's V3 speaking-TTS project, which he said in June 2025 was still unshipped). The 2026 music/live-performance layer is much better sourced: the 19 December 2026 extended-reality concert at The Vermont Hollywood, Los Angeles (12:19 PST, with a live band, marking Neuro's fourth birthday) is confirmed at T2 on Vedal's own vedal.ai and neurosama.com/concert, and Pollstar/BroadwayWorld add the 21 September on-sale, the mid-November EPs full_code and do_not_reboot, and a direct Vedal quote that the live turn "isn't necessarily a move away from Twitch". The strongest pipeline evidence is that released tracks are demonstrably not raw live-synthesis dumps: QueenPB is credited with vocal production, VocaDB describes her as providing "tuning" for many Neuro and Evil songs, and "Chinatown Blues" exists as both a 2024 original and a separately catalogued December 2025 remaster, with rpwithai documenting refined vocals and improved instrumentals versus the karaoke original. What remains genuinely unknown is the thing that matters most for a backend report: whether any of this is real-time singing-voice synthesis at all, and specifically whether the concert's vocals will be live SVS in the venue or pre-rendered, QueenPB-tuned stems synced to the avatars — no official, news or Vedal source addresses it, and vedal.ai's separate "Instrumentals Download" page is the only hint that vocal and instrumental stems are handled independently. Finally, every RVC / so-vits-svc / AI-cover-generator attribution for Neuro's singing is fan-tool contamination and must be kept out of the report.

## Summary B (skeptic)
Everything firmly established in this cluster is about events and credits, not about how the singing actually works. At T2, Vedal's own site and neurosama.com/concert confirm the 19 December 2026 show at The Vermont Hollywood, Los Angeles at 12:19pm PST with a live band, billed as their "first extended-reality live concert" celebrating Neuro's fourth birthday, tickets at neuroevil.eventbrite.com — but the press framing of it as a first-ever live, in-person debut is contradicted by Neuro and Evil performing on stage at Bilibili World in Shanghai on 11 July 2026. The full official platform set (Twitch plus a Japanese Twitch channel, YouTube, X, TikTok, Bilibili, Discord, Reddit, two Spotify artist pages, shop/Taobao, GitHub, the Swarm Sync app) is confirmed at T2 from vedal.ai's own link list. The two EPs, full_code (Neuro) and do_not_reboot (Evil), are corroborated for "mid-November 2026" by Pollstar and BroadwayWorld — effectively one press release, so closer to T4 than T3 — with the precise 16 November date resting only on Wikipedia. On the pipeline itself, both outlets carry the identical sentence "synthetically produced vocals … with vocal production by QueenPB", which names a person and never an engine and never says whether synthesis is live or pre-rendered; the only engine named anywhere ("Synthesizer V Studio Pro 2", on NamuWiki's uncited, machine-translated song subpage) is anachronistic against its own June-2023 "V3 singing voice" date and must not be reported as fact. What is reasonably established architecturally is only that karaoke is a subsystem separate from the chat/LLM loop: two independent wiki communities say so and describe the same early "thanks donators mid-song" concurrency bug, and Vedal's own 28 April 2026 rewrite stream lists "duets in karaoke" as a module still to be reimplemented (T1) — while the V1/V2/V3 singing-generation labels, though independently corroborated by NamuWiki's per-song tagging of hundreds of covers, carry no citation and must never be conflated with the Live2D avatar's own V1/V2/V3 labels. There is concrete but T5-only evidence of an offline human pass on released tracks (QueenPB refining vocals, the Astreoid Music Team redoing instrumentals on the 2025 licensed official covers), and nothing at all on what happens on concert night. Two dates in the claim set are simply wrong: the first human-streamer collab was 6 February 2023, not 22 February, and Neuro's 3D debut was not the pair's first 3D move, since Evil's 3D model shipped 26 June 2025; and every RVC / so-vits-svc "Neuro V3" model surfaced by singing searches is a third-party fan cover generator, not Vedal's stack.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction #5 (first collab date) — RESOLVED in favour of 6 February 2023. Wikipedia places the first human-streamer collab only 'In February' 2023 without a day; contemporaneous evidence (a clip titled 'Neuro-sama - 6th Feb - First Collab w/ Miyune in Minecraft' and Know Your Meme's account of the AIWorks edit uploaded 6 Feb 2023) fixes it at 6 Feb, which also makes the 2-day STT interruption-fix story (Camila collab, 8 Feb 2023) coherent. Angle 09's '22 February 2023' is wrong and claim 09-timeline-2026#06 has been corrected accordingly.
- Contradiction #6 ('V3' label collision) — RESOLVED as four non-commensurable version tracks. For this cluster the relevant ones are: (a) the SINGING version track, which NamuWiki dates to a 'V3 singing voice' released June 2023 and which the (uncited) Fandom Karaoke page labels V1/V2/V3; (b) the Live2D AVATAR track, whose V3 model shipped 19 December 2024; and (c) Vedal's SPEAKING-TTS 'V3 voice' project, which he said on stream in June 2025 was still not good enough to ship. These are three different subsystems that happen to share the label. Any report sentence using 'V3' about Neuro must name the subsystem.
- Clone-contamination flag #5 (RVC / so-vits-svc for singing) — UPHELD as contamination. Every RVC/so-vits-svc/AI-cover hit (FakeYou, Jammable, VoiceDub, Storyteller, Singify) is a third-party consumer voice-clone service trained on Neuro's output, never a description of Vedal's pipeline. The only named human in the real vocal chain is QueenPB, described by VocaDB as providing 'tuning' — a synthesised-vocal post-production role, which is evidence about post-processing, not about the synthesis engine.
- Clone-contamination flag re: kimjammer/Neuro (claim r2-07#11) — UPHELD by direct check of the repo README ('The goal of this project was to recreate Neuro-Sama, but only running on local models on consumer hardware'; LLAMA 3 8B EXL2, MiniCPM-Llama3-V, faster_whisper tiny.en, CoquiTTS/XTTSv2, VTube Studio). None of its components may be cited as Neuro's.

As the voice was updated to V3, the processing of high and low sounds was stabilized." The singing V1/V2/V3 is therefore a voice-generation track distinct from the Live2D avatar V1 (2022) / V2 (27 May 2023) / V3 (19 Dec 2024) numbering and from the separate 'V3 voice' TTS effort Vedal described in June 2025. NamuWiki dates the singing V3 to June 2023; the Fandom Karaoke page gives no dates at all. Report the three tracks separately and never as one version number.
- Clone-contamination flag #5 (RVC / so-vits-svc as Neuro's singing stack) — CONFIRMED as contamination. Every RVC/so-vits artefact surfaced by singing-engine searches is a third-party fan cover generator trained on her voice (jammable.com/custom-neuro-sama-v3, voicedub.ai, voice-models.com 'Neuro-sama V3 (rvmpe, RVC v2, 250 Epochs)', singify, fakeyou). None is connected to Vedal, and the 'V3' in those product names is borrowed fan labelling, not evidence about his pipeline.
- Clone-contamination note r2-07#11 (kimjammer/Neuro) — UPHELD; kimjammer's 'Neuro dev log' series is a fan recreation and must not be cited as evidence about the singing or any other Vedal subsystem.
