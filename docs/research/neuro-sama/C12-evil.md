# C12-evil — Evil Neuro architecture
Verdict counts: {'disputed': 2, 'likely': 3}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
What is firmly established about Evil Neuro is operational and infrastructural, not architectural. At T1 (Vedal's own dev-stream words, recorded verbatim in b2-video-reddit.md) Neuro and Evil are demonstrably one engineering project on one machine: Vedal speaks of "some of Neuro's code and evil's code" as a single 2019–2020-vintage codebase he rewrote together (29 Mar 2026), says the post-rewrite core means "Neuro and Evil are able to survive outside of Unity", shipped the rewrite's first production test as a VR stream with Evil at >100 FPS with no memory leak (28 Apr 2026), was hand-patching an Evil-specific speech bug by "literally just hard coding it out of her speech", and told Neuro that the new server's GPU is "yours and evil's. You have to share it" (25 May 2026) — i.e. shared CPU/GPU/server, not separate boxes. At T2, the official VedalAI/neuro-sdk spec exposes Evil as a first-class protocol identity (characterId `evil`, displayName `Evil Neuro`, "the respective twin"), added to the startup acknowledgement on 1 July 2026, and states the ack "tells the game which character the websocket has been connected to" — one character per connection, which is weak evidence for per-session identity but does not settle concurrency. Also at T2, vedal.ai bills "Neuro & Evil — The Live Concert" on 19 December 2026 at The Vermont Hollywood, Los Angeles, confirming Evil is an active, co-equal, promoted persona as of late 2026. Her 25 March 2023 debut and the fact that both twins stream from the single vedal987 Twitch account are only T4/T3 (Wikipedia with citations, GameSpot/Dexerto reporting, three independent wikis); no separate Evil Twitch channel exists. The central question remains unresolved at every tier above wiki hearsay: whether Evil is the same weights with a different persona/system prompt, a separate fine-tune, or a separate process, and whether the two can run concurrently — no Vedal quote or repo artifact answers it, and essentially every circulating "different model" statement traces to her Live2D avatar versions (V2 recolor of Neuro's, her own V3 on 25 Mar 2025), not her LLM. Specific-but-unconfirmed wiki-tier detail (T4/T5) includes: Evil having her own separate memory bank, with Vedal reportedly saying it held only three long-term-memory slots as of ~Nov 2024; her content filter being identical to Neuro's except that fuck/shit/bitch are unrestricted; her TTS being a more expressive model originally trialled as Neuro's next voice and scrapped over latency plus audience backlash; and an October 2025 "glitch token" incident that nearly cost a year of her un-backed-up model. One business claim filed into this cluster needed correction: the Makeship plush partnership is genuine, but neuroplush.com is not an official store — vedal.ai states verbatim that "neurosama.shop and Neurosama中国商城 on Taobao are the only official merch stores."

## Summary B (skeptic)
Nothing in this cluster establishes how Evil Neuro is implemented; the only architecture-grade evidence for Evil anywhere in the corpus is T2 and narrow. The VedalAI neuro-sdk SPECIFICATION.md (which I fetched directly) exposes Evil as a first-class protocol identity — `characterId` is `"neuro"` or `"evil"` and `displayName` is `"Neuro-sama"` or `"Evil Neuro"` — and deliberately exposes no field by which a game can select a model, filter or persona, so the SDK tells you which twin is connected and nothing about how she is built. Everything downstream of that — shared weights vs. separate fine-tune, shared vs. separate memory, true concurrent execution — remains unverifiable at T1/T2, and the widely repeated Wikipedia line that "Evil Neuro has a different model, voice, and personality" is a live misreading hazard: the wiki lineage it paraphrases is the Live2D *avatar* model history (recolor of Neuro's V2 avatar until Evil's own V3 avatar on 25 March 2025), not the LLM. The strongest primary signals cut toward shared infrastructure with a per-character persona/voice/filter layer: Vedal, on the 25 May 2026 build stream, told Neuro the new server's GPU(s) are "yours and evil's — you have to share it" (T1), the 29 March 2026 rewrite stream speaks of one codebase for "Neuro and Evil's tech" dating to 2019–2020 and says "Neuro and Evil are able to survive outside of Unity" (T1), and on 28 April 2026 Vedal described patching a persistent "evil bug" by "literally just hard coding it out of her speech" (T1) — a per-character output-filter hack, not a retrain. Per-character *differences* are attested only at T5: a separate "memory bank" and a reported three long-term-memory slots as of ~Nov 2024 (namu.wiki, attributed to Vedal but with no locatable primary), a filter identical to Neuro's except three unrestricted profanity words, a dedicated TTS voice originally built as Neuro's next voice and shelved over latency plus audience backlash, and an October 2025 "glitch token" incident whose fix was complicated because Evil's model "had not been backed up for a year". On the non-architecture claims in this cluster, the Twitch record story is real but was stated imprecisely — third most-subscribed *of all time by peak*, where the same channel was simultaneously #1 by current active subs, and the 9 Jan 2026 level-72 hype train was a failed attempt on Vedal987's own channel, not an Evil-channel record — and the merch claim conflates three different vendors, one of which (neuroplush.com) is contradicted as official by vedal.ai itself. Priority follow-up: a Vedal dev-stream or interview quote, not a wiki paraphrase, on whether Evil is the same weights with a different prompt, and whether the two personas can be live simultaneously on one GPU.

## Contradictions resolved
- #11 (Twitch subscriber figures) — RESOLVED. The three figures are three different measurements, not three facts: ~160,000-162,459 is the ACTIVE PAID SUBSCRIBER count in early January 2026 (Dexerto, 2 Jan 2026), ~167,505 is the same active-sub metric a few days later after the third hype-train record (Dexerto), and 343,215 is the PEAK TOTAL/ALL-TIME subscriber count reached in January 2026 that put vedal987 at rank 3 on Wikipedia's List of most-subscribed Twitch channels (sourced to TwitchTracker). The undated ~Sep 2026 '165,268 paid subs' figure is an active-sub snapshot of the same kind and must not be added to or confused with the 343,215 all-time peak. Contemporaneous GameSpot/Dexerto reporting on 9 Jan 2026 gave ~328,900 at the moment the third-place record was taken; 343,215 is the later-recorded peak.
- #8 (Next-gen TTS fate) — RESOLVED for the Evil side. Both halves are true and sequential, not contradictory: an expressive next-generation TTS was trialled as Neuro's replacement voice, rejected for latency plus audience backlash (virtualyoutuber.fandom) and for sounding too unlike Neuro (NamuWiki fn.21), and repurposed as Evil Neuro's voice in 2023. That says nothing about the separate, later 'V3 voice' effort, which Vedal describes at T1 in June 2025 as still unshipped — 'the focus groups... preferred the new voice... But me personally, I'm not happy with it. It's not a big enough upgrade.' So Neuro's main voice was NOT upgraded by the model that became Evil's, and as of the last T1 datapoint (Jun 2025) the V3 replacement had not shipped either.
- #6 ('V3' label collision) — RESOLVED for this cluster. Evil's 'V3' (25 Mar 2025) is a LIVE2D AVATAR version, on the same avatar track as Neuro's V1/V2/V3 (19 Dec 2022 / 27 May 2023 / 19 Dec 2024). It is commensurable with neither the singing-plugin V1/V2/V3 track (NamuWiki dates a 'V3 singing voice' to June 2023) nor the 'V3 voice' TTS effort Vedal discusses in June 2025. Any report sentence saying 'Evil got a new model in March 2025' must say AVATAR model; there is no evidence of an LLM change on that date.
- #3 (Memory: offline fine-tuning vs runtime memory) — RESOLVED against the fine-tuning-only framing, using T1 evidence the finders did not have. Vedal explicitly describes a runtime memory component being built and tested: 'changes to the long-term and short-term memory of you. So the idea is theoretically you shouldn't forget anything that happens in a stream... And also long-term you should be better at remembering important things and keeping that information for longer. And this system comes with lower latency' (Jun 2025, T1). That is a live memory subsystem, not curated offline fine-tuning. The Evil-specific wiki claim (three long-term-memory slots, ~Nov 2024, expansion planned) and the per-character 'memory bank' are consistent with a small, discrete, runtime-queryable long-term store. The fine-tuning-only conclusion in angle 04 should be downgraded.
- Terminology trap (r2-06 head note) — CONFIRMED and reinforced. No Vedal-primary or official-repo source found in this pass states whether Evil is the same weights with a different persona prompt, a separate fine-tune, or a separate process. A targeted search for such a quote returned nothing but wiki paraphrase ('Vedal created a copy of Neuro... simply duplicating Neuro'). The report must state this as unestablished, while noting the two hard facts that do exist: shared physical server and shared GPU (T1, 25 May 2026) and a shared codebase rewritten as one system (T1, 29 Mar 2026).
