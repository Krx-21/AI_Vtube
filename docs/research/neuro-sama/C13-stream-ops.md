Verdict counts: {'confirmed': 1, 'likely': 9, 'disputed': 1}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
Nothing in this cluster touches Neuro's model internals; it is almost entirely audience-scale and platform-operations material, and its evidence quality is correspondingly shallow. Only one claim reaches primary tier: the Neuro Game SDK specification (T2, VedalAI repo) verbatim confirms that action schemas must be `type: "object"` and lists roughly thirty JSON-Schema keywords — $ref, $defs, allOf/anyOf/oneOf, not, if/then/else, additionalProperties, patternProperties, min/maxProperties, even title and description — as "probably not supported well," which is strong evidence that Vedal's schema-to-action layer is a bespoke, simplified constrained-generation translator rather than a standard validator. The subathon/hype-train/subscriber claims are all T3–T4 secondary: three hype-train world records (level 111 on 1 Jan 2025, a second in Dec 2025, level 126 on 5 Jan 2026) are corroborated by Wikipedia, Dexerto and Stream Hatchet independently, and vedal987's 343,215 peak / third-all-time ranking appears in Wikipedia's most-subscribed-channels table as well as its Neuro article; Dexerto (2 Jan 2026) separately documents 162,459 active subs versus Jynxzi's 73,942. Exact per-train subscriber counts are NOT reliable — Wikipedia, Streams Charts and Dexerto give 118,989, 167,505 and 296,482 for overlapping events because they count different windows — so the report should cite levels and dates, not sub totals. Two subathon-mechanics claims were misattributed: the cited rpwithai article (published 20 Dec 2025, and about the third subathon) contains neither the timer-mechanics quote nor the 262.5-hour figure; those numbers actually come from virtualyoutuber.fandom, which ties the 262.5 hours to one record hype train rather than to all donations. The "stream length cut from 9 hours to 2–3 hours for maintenance" claim is a selective reading — Wikipedia gives three reasons (boredom, viewer overload, maintenance) — so it cannot carry an argument about engineering upkeep cost. What remains genuinely unknown: how many chat messages Neuro ingests per turn or how they are prioritized, whether the subathon timer is wired into Vedal's own software or an off-the-shelf overlay, the mechanism of the collab voice-input (STT) path, and whether the @NeurosamaAI posting pipeline is autonomous or queued for approval — no primary source addresses any of these.
