# C08-avatar — Avatar, Live2D, Unity/VTube Studio, embodiment
Verdict counts: {'disputed': 9, 'unverifiable': 7, 'likely': 14, 'refuted': 2, 'confirmed': 3}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
What is firmly established for this cluster is the avatar production chain and the fact that Unity sits at the heart of Neuro's stack. Wikipedia, plus the illustrator's and rigger's own posts, give a consistent T3/T4 record: a stock Live2D 'Hiyori Momose' model at the 19 Dec 2022 debut, a first original avatar on 27 May 2023 (design Anny, rig Otozuki Teru, Vedal as programmer), a third Live2D model on 19 Dec 2024 (design Anny, rig Kitanya), and a separate 3D model by jjinomu introduced 15 Nov 2025 — that 3D model is an additional asset, not a "V4" of the Live2D line, and the report should drop that label. The single biggest upgrade in this pass is architectural: Vedal's own dev streams (29 Mar and 28 Apr 2026, the latter republished by Neuro-sama's official clips channel) raise "the avatar runs in the Unity game engine" from a Wikipedia line to T1, and go further — chronic Unity memory leaks were the stated main reason for the 2026 codebase rewrite, and after it "Neuro and Evil are able to survive outside of Unity… Unity can be restarted perfectly fine", i.e. Unity is now a restartable render/3D layer rather than the host of core logic. That kills the clone-derived "TTS into VTube Studio over a virtual audio cable" picture: VTube Studio's Event API and .exp3.json expression system are real (T2, DenchiSoft's own repo) but are documentation about the product, not about Neuro. Two finder claims of absence were wrong and are corrected here: NamuWiki does document a control-panel function that forcibly blocks Neuro's speech (rarely used since the early-2023 filter reform), and the NeuroCar and Evil's drone are demonstrated physical-embodiment bits (the NeuroCar was driven by Neuro at Open Sauce 2025), not title-only rumours — while the Neurodog's reveal date is disputed between 9 Feb 2024 (wiki) and a January 2025 video cluster. What remains genuinely unknown is everything about the brain: the "~2B parameters / q2_k" figure traces to a single uncited Fandom sentence with no archived stream behind it and should be reported as unverifiable lore (and is implausible against the May 2026 Threadripper/RTX PRO 6000 server); the Dec 2022 "GPT-3 API" origin is contradicted by the Airis-language-model account and by NamuWiki's GPT-2 guess; whether personality lives in weights or a prompt is unresolved by any primary source; and no Vedal statement or VedalAI repo says whether Evil Neuro is separate weights, a LoRA, a prompt variant or a separate process — the SDK spec distinguishes only characterId "neuro" vs "evil". Finally, one clean T1 win outside the avatar core: Vedal's Pollstar quote about designing "how performers can exist in a venue" is verbatim and dated 21 Sep 2026, attached to the 19 Dec 2026 extended-reality concert at The Vermont Hollywood.

## Summary B (skeptic)
What is solidly established about Neuro-sama's embodiment is the avatar lineage and its credits, at wiki-plus-first-party tier (T3/T4): a stock Live2D "Hiyori Momose" model in 2022; a custom V2 on 27 May 2023 designed by Anny, rigged by Otozuki Teru (confirmed by the rigger's own X post) and stated by Wikipedia to run in the Unity game engine; a V3 on 19 Dec 2024 designed by Anny and rigged by Kitanya; Evil Neuro's own independent Live2D model on 25 Mar 2025; and a separate 3D model by jjinomu introduced 15 Nov 2025, later used in VRChat. The single most load-bearing architecture claim in this cluster — that the avatar lives in a bespoke Unity build rather than the consumer VTube Studio app — is half-right and now dated: Wikipedia supports Unity only for V2, the Live2D Cubism SDK detail is pure inference, and Vedal's own Mar–Apr 2026 dev streams (T1) show he deliberately rewrote the character systems *out* of Unity because of years of unfixable memory leaks, leaving Unity as a restartable rendering layer ("Neuro and Evil are able to survive outside of Unity"). Several citations in this cluster do not support their claims: the HandWiki "Python orchestration" quote is not on HandWiki, and the Wikipedia article contains nothing about hallucinations, a repeated "technical difficulties" line, or the filter-scope evolution attributed to it. Two negative findings were overturned — channel-point redeems are confirmed as a real backend feature by Vedal himself ("redeems aren't implemented" in the rewrite), a control-panel speech kill-switch is documented at wiki tier, and NeuroCar is a real project that drove at Open Sauce 2025 on 20 July 2025 — leaving AI-driven OBS scene switching as the only genuine gap of the three. The revenue/subscriber items are weaker than presented: 165,268 subs and ">$400,000/month" are one January 2026 snapshot plus one Dexerto estimate that Futurism merely repeats, not a September 2026 measurement or two independent confirmations. Everything about the LLM itself remains unknown: the "~2B parameters / q2_k" figure traces to a single uncited Fandom sentence (and is stale next to the May 2026 server), the Dec 2022 "GPT-3 API" origin has no primary source and conflicts with the better-cited "Airis' language model" account, and whether Evil shares Neuro's weights is undocumented — the official SDK only establishes that the protocol treats them as two characters, `neuro` and `evil`. The report must keep Live2D "model" and LLM "model" strictly apart; Evil's independent avatar (25 Mar 2025) says nothing about her brain, and at least one low-quality blog has already mis-dated her debut because of exactly that confusion.
