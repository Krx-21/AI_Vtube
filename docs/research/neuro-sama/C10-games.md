# C10-games — Game integrations
Verdict counts: {'disputed': 14, 'confirmed': 7, 'unverifiable': 6, 'likely': 9, 'refuted': 1}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
What is solidly established for game integrations sits at T1–T2. The official Neuro SDK (VedalAI/neuro-sdk; the old github.com/VedalAI/neuro-game-sdk URL now redirects there) is a first-party plaintext-JSON WebSocket protocol with official Unity and Godot SDKs, and its README (last updated 1 Jul 2026) states verbatim that it is "optimized for turn-based games, namely Inscryption, Liar's Bar and Buckshot Roulette," that "games that require a non-low APM will generally not work well without Neuro only controlling the high-level actions," and lists Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, Uno, Monopoly, most visual novels and most turn-based card games as good fits; the published example repos are Slay the Spire 2 (neuro-sts2 — verified as a real mod for the commercial Godot sequel, not a naming mix-up), Inscryption, Buckshot Roulette, Hollow Knight, Cyberpunk 2077 and Pokémon Platinum, with neuro-liarsbar and neuro-millionaire also present in the org. Vedal's own 22 Feb 2025 comment in discussion #58 is genuine T1 and says Neuro "would independently and simultaneously process both requests at once and the two states should be merged back afterwards" — but he prefixes it with "I really don't think you should do this," adds "Neuro can't think about two things at once," calls it untested, and states plainly that a context message arriving mid-decision "won't affect her action decision and instead the context will be merged after she is finished," so the load-bearing architecture fact is a strictly serial decision step, not parallel reasoning. A newly surfaced T1 source, Vedal's 1 Jul 2024 interview on osu!'s own news site, reframes the origin story: he says he "was just getting started with learning AI in 2019," that "the original AI took a few days to make," that the AI-VTuber idea came "two years later" and the merger only "in early 2022," and that "AIs like ChatGPT are trained to act as assistants and tools for humans, but Neuro is trained differently" — the strongest primary support yet for personality-by-training, and a direct challenge to the near-universal "created in 2018" date. Two long-standing claims fail: the JimenezLi Minecraft mod is a fan tribute mod that adds Neuro-themed content to Minecraft (2 stars, last touched June 2023), not the mechanism by which Neuro plays it, and the Neuropilot/Neuroscope split is not a hypothetical design pattern — Neuro really did pilot the ship in Outer Wilds via a Vedal-made autopilot mod on stream. The bespoke, pre-SDK Among Us plugin is official T2 and explicitly state-based ("record data from the game, which will then be used to train a neural network which Neuro will use"), which together with the 80x60 monochrome Python osu! model shows Neuro's game AIs have always been per-game and either state-trained or pixel-trained, never one general vision model feeding the LLM. What remains unknown: how Minecraft is actually controlled (the 22 Jul 2024 dev stream describes it as an open real-time-AI research problem, and the osu! bot was still on "an old codebase" as of 27 Jul 2026); the mechanism behind chess, Geoguessr, Keep Talking and Golf It; whether Neuro's "80x60 grayscale" input ever extended beyond osu!; and every claim that system prompts carry only situational context, which has no primary source at all.

## Summary B (skeptic)
What is solidly established for game integrations comes almost entirely from the VedalAI GitHub org (T2). The Neuro Game SDK is a first-party plaintext-JSON WebSocket protocol with official Unity and Godot packages, explicitly "created and optimized for turn-based games, namely Inscryption, Liar's Bar and Buckshot Roulette", explicitly warning that games needing non-low APM "will generally not work well without Neuro only controlling the high-level actions and letting another system decide how to handle the low-level actions", and explicitly requiring the whole game state to be describable in text — all verified verbatim against the README (last updated 1 July 2026). A direct listing of the org's 16 repositories fixes the true integration set: Slay the Spire 2 (neuro-sts2, a genuine mod for the commercial sequel, actively maintained to Aug 2026, with multiplayer support), Inscryption, Buckshot Roulette, Hollow Knight, Cyberpunk 2077, Pokémon Platinum, plus Liar's Bar (now deprecated over the game's Mono-to-IL2CPP switch), Who Wants To Be A Millionaire (a bespoke pre-SDK websocket protocol) and swarm-control (a Twitch-extension crowd-control stack for Subnautica). The single most important correction for the report is that the popular "Neuro plays everything through the SDK" framing is false: osu!, Minecraft, Among Us and chess are NOT SDK games, and the Among Us agent can be inspected at code level — VedalAI/neuro-amongus trains a PyTorch LSTM over structured game-state vectors (velocity, task and sabotage positions, eight raycast obstacle distances), with no pixel input at all, which directly undercuts the widely-copied "computer vision modules convert on-screen data to structured text" formulation whose only source is an uncited AI-generated aggregator. Second-tier facts rest on encyclopaedia/wiki sourcing (T4): the 2018 osu!-only origin and ~3,000 followers in 2019, the 19 December 2022 relaunch, the mrekk 1v1 win reported by Vedal but ranked only on a private (Akatsuki) server rather than Bancho, the 6 February 2023 Miyune Minecraft collab, Slay the Spire from 8 January 2023, and the December 2025 hardcore-Minecraft marathon completed on attempt 87. What remains genuinely unknown is how Neuro actually controls Minecraft (the community repo previously cited for this is a fan tribute mod, not a control hook), what mechanism drives chess, GeoGuessr and Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, whether Neuro has ever streamed Outer Wilds with the real third-party NeuroPilot/NeuroScope mods, and whether the osu! agent's wiki-described 80x60 monochrome Python screen reader is still current — the wiki itself notes that as of 27 July 2026 the osu! bot was still running on "an old codebase", i.e. apparently untouched by the Mar–Jun 2026 rewrite that moved Neuro and Evil out of Unity. No primary Vedal statement was found for any per-game vision or control detail in this cluster.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction 2 (action-force concurrency) — RESOLVED. All three statements are real and compatible once scoped. Vedal987 (neuro-sdk discussion #58, 22 Feb 2025) says the decision step itself is strictly serial: "she can only process one thing at once. This means it won't affect her action decision and instead the context will be merged after she is finished making her decision," and he explicitly states "Neuro can't think about two things at once." The parallel-and-merge sentence is from the same comment but is hedged three ways — "I really don't think you should do this", "should try", "this is untested" — and it describes the wrapper attempting two independent force-handling runs whose states are reconciled afterwards, not concurrent reasoning. The SPECIFICATION's "Neuro can only handle one action force at a time... will cause problems" is the documented, supported contract. Report the serial model as the architecture and the parallel-merge line as untested intended behaviour that Vedal discourages.
- Contradiction 5 (first collab date) — RESOLVED in favour of 6 February 2023. A dated clip titled "Neuro-sama - 6th Feb - First Collab w/ Miyune in Minecraft" and the fandom Miyune page both put the first-ever collab on 6 Feb 2023 with Miyune in Minecraft. The 22 February 2023 date carried by angle 09 is not the first human-streamer collab and should be dropped or re-labelled as a later Miyune stream. The 2-day STT-fix story (Camila collab, 8 Feb 2023) therefore remains chronologically possible but is itself wiki-only and unverified.
- Contradiction 10 (80x60 game-vision misattribution) — RESOLVED, and angle 01 was right to be cautious. Three independent wikis carry the figure, but namu.wiki (KR and EN) scopes it precisely to the osu! model — "The osu!-playing game model is written in Python and recognizes 80x60-pixel monochannel images to move" — with the stated rationale that monochrome avoids three-channel RGB training cost. Only virtualyoutuber.fandom extends it to Minecraft. Meanwhile the official VedalAI Among Us plugin trains on recorded game state, and the SDK is text-in/text-out. Conclusion: attribute 80x60 monochrome to osu! (possibly early Minecraft), never as a general input format, and drop the widespread 'converted to structured text for the LLM' phrasing entirely — it comes from aggregator copy and contradicts the pixel-to-movement description.
- Contradiction 12 (early history / Airis / 2021 prototype) — LARGELY RESOLVED. Vedal's own 1 Jul 2024 osu! interview supplies the connective tissue: he built the osu! AI while "just getting started with learning AI in 2019", had the AI-VTuber idea "two years later" (matching the 5 Aug 2021 private Airis demo recorded by neurosama.fandom with a cited clip), and only "in early 2022" decided to combine the two. So 'Airis' and the '2021 prototype' are the same system, it IS the direct ancestor of the December 2022 relaunch, and the merger date is early-to-March 2022. The remaining unresolved piece is the creation year: press and Wikipedia say 2018, Vedal's own words say 2019 — report it as 2018–2019 with Vedal's 2019 figure flagged as the primary-source date. The separate low-confidence 'GPT-2/GPT-3-era tech in the 2021 prototype' claim gains no support and stays unverifiable speculation.
- Contradiction 4 (personality: weights vs prompt) — PARTIALLY RESOLVED, and the medium-confidence grade in angles 03/09 should be split. Vedal's T1 statement to osu! — "AIs like ChatGPT are trained to act as assistants and tools for humans, but Neuro is trained differently", alongside "the hardest part of making Neuro was giving her personality" — does establish at primary-source level that personality comes from how she is trained, not from prompting alone. But nothing primary supports the second half of the framing (that system prompts were reduced to situational context only), nor the mid-2023 date, nor a preceding GPT-3 prompt-only era. Split the claim: 'personality instilled by training' = likely/T1-supported; 'system prompts carry only situational context' = unverifiable, with angle 10's finding that community sources disagree standing.
- Contradiction 2 (action-force concurrency) — RESOLVED. The official spec's 'Neuro can only handle one action force at a time... will cause problems' and Vedal's discussion-#58 'parallel then merge' are not equal-weight statements. Vedal's own comment (22 Feb 2025) opens 'I really don't think you should do this', flags the parallel path as 'untested', and in the SAME comment states 'she can only process one thing at once' for context arriving mid-decision. The reportable position: the decision step is serial with queued/merged context; optimistic parallel-force handling is a stated design intent that Vedal himself says is untested and discourages.
- Contradiction 5 (first collab date) — RESOLVED in favour of 6 February 2023. A contemporaneous clip is titled 'Neuro-sama - 6th Feb - First Collab w/ Miyune in Minecraft', Miyune's own clip is captioned 'WORLD'S FIRST AI x VTUBER COLLAB', and Wikipedia places a Miyune Minecraft collab in February 2023. Angle 09's '22 February 2023 first human-streamer collab' is not supported and should be dropped or re-scoped. The dependent '8 Feb Camila fixed the interruption bug' story remains single-wiki and unverifiable (the fandom page now returns HTTP 402).
- Contradiction 10 (game-vision misattribution) — RESOLVED in favour of angle 06's retraction request. The 80x60 monochrome figure is attached by every traceable wiki (neurosama.fandom, namu.wiki KR/EN, virtualyoutuber.fandom) to the osu!-PLAYING model specifically, written in Python, with namu.wiki giving the engineering rationale that monochrome avoids 3-channel RGB training cost. Generalising it to 'Neuro's game input handling' is unsupported, and the accompanying 'converted to structured text for the LLM' clause is not in the Dexerto article it is credited to — it comes from futureaiblog.com (T6). For the one game whose agent can actually be inspected (Among Us), the input is structured engine state, not pixels.
- Contradiction 12 (early history / Airis) — PARTIALLY RESOLVED. The browser-scraped neurosama.fandom Airis page gives a reference-backed chain: Vedal built Airis while working on Abandoned Archive, posted a private YouTube demo on 5 August 2021 (chat filter shown), and around March 2022 decided to combine Airis with the old osu! AI 'to give her goals and a better sense of purpose'. virtualyoutuber.fandom independently says Airis is 'currently Neuro's internal code name'. So 'Airis' and the '2021 prototype' are the same system and it IS the direct ancestor of the December 2022 relaunch. The separate claim that this prototype used GPT-2/GPT-3-era technology remains explicitly hedged as a guess by two namu.wiki editors ('추정상 GPT 2' / 'presumably GPT-2') and must not be reported as a disclosure.
- Clone-contamination flag 9 (Neuropilot/Neuroscope) — RESOLVED, but in the opposite direction from the flag. These are not merely a Japanese dev blog's illustrative pattern: Hawkbat/NeuroPilot is a real released Outer Wilds mod built on the official Neuro Game API that exposes ship autopilot to Neuro and disables manual control, with NeuroScope credited as a companion telemetry component. The flag's substantive point still stands — they are integrator-side third-party mods and reveal nothing about Vedal's backend — but calling them a hypothetical design pattern is factually wrong.
- Contradiction 4 (personality location) — RESOLVED as UNVERIFIABLE, siding with angle 10. Both the 03 and 09 'personality baked into weights, system prompts only for situational context' claims trace to one and the same passage in the lin-guanguo community page ('The Core Question: Weights vs. Prompts / Vedal's Stated Approach'), with no archived stream link or timestamp; the same author elsewhere demands exactly that standard before believing community lore. Hugging Face forum consensus argues the opposite (a personality-anchor system prompt). The medium-confidence grade in 03/09 is not defensible; report this as an open question, not an architecture fact.
