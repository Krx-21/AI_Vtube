# C07-vision — Vision subsystem
Verdict counts: {'unverifiable': 7, 'disputed': 3, 'likely': 1, 'confirmed': 2}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
Almost nothing about Neuro's vision subsystem is documented at T1/T2 except its behaviour and its constraints. The strongest evidence in the whole cluster is Vedal's own words on the 29 September 2025 dev stream (clip transcript, T1): Neuro's vision is "kind of stunted right now for the sake of latency", he "know[s] how to do it better", and there is a separate manual path — "if I take a screenshot and then send it to you on Discord" — implying at least two distinct image pipelines of different fidelity. At T2, the official VedalAI neuro-sdk README establishes that the SDK game-integration path is text-only ("Since you need to describe the entire game state in text, and receive actions in text..."), so SDK games involve no vision at all; the only game-vision figure in circulation (Python, 80x60 monochannel/grayscale screen input) is corroborated by three wikis but belongs to the osu!/game-playing AI, not to general image understanding. No source at any tier names the model, API, sampling rate, or in-house-vs-third-party status of the general vision/VLM path — this is a genuine public unknown, not a search failure. The only named vision model in circulation, MiniCPM-Llama3-V-2_5-int4, is confirmed from the kimjammer/Neuro repository to belong to that clone project, and search engines were observed actively misattributing it to Vedal during this verification pass, so the report must state explicitly that Neuro's vision model is unnamed. The widely-cited "vision latency up to five seconds" figure rests on a single uncited July-2024 Fandom dev-stream recap (T5) and should be reported as unverified, even though the underlying latency-versus-fidelity tradeoff it describes is independently confirmed by Vedal at T1. The claim that vision and speech "cannot operate simultaneously" is misattributed to Wikipedia — it comes from Wu & Lingel (2025, New Media & Society), an observational media-studies paper whose full text is paywalled — and is better read as low-fidelity vision than as an architectural mutual exclusion. Finally, the "~2B parameters / q2_k" LLM lore remains unverifiable and is plausibly back-formed from clone-builder culture, and all of these figures predate the April 2026 codebase rewrite and May 2026 server rebuild, so any 2024-2025 latency number should be labelled as superseded-status-unknown for 2026.

## Summary B (skeptic)
Almost nothing about Neuro's vision subsystem is established at T1/T2. The only primary (T1) evidence is Vedal's own commentary in the captcha dev-stream clip (youtube.com/watch?v=rp5FKZej-cg, uploaded 7 Oct 2025), where he says her vision is "kind of stunted right now for the sake of latency" and reveals a separate manual path — "if I take a screenshot and then send it to you on Discord" — i.e. a deliberately latency-capped live vision path plus an ad-hoc image path, with no model, resolution or sampling rate named. That captcha test belongs to the 29 Sep 2025 dev stream, confirmed by the official Neuro-sama channel's own VOD title "Neuro-sama | 29 Sep 2025 | Development & I'm Not a Robot"; the rival 22 Jul 2024 date belongs to a different dev stream whose fan-wiki recap mentions vision latency but no captcha, so that part of the record is a date confusion, not two separate tests. At T2, the official neuro-game-sdk specification is explicitly text-only ("An arbitrary string that describes the current state of the game"), with no image, screenshot or video channel anywhere — so SDK-integrated games are not a vision path at all. Everything else is wiki-tier or worse: the "up to five seconds" image-processing latency figure exists only in one uncited Fandom dev-stream recap (Fandom returns HTTP 402 to this environment, and every search hit merely echoes that same page), and it is in any case superseded by the Sep 2025 "stunted for latency" state and by the Mar–Jun 2026 rewrite plus the May 2026 server. The "80x60 grayscale" figure is described by three wikis as the input to the Python game-playing AI (osu!/Minecraft), not to general image vision, and must not be generalised. The only vision model ever named in this record, MiniCPM-Llama3-V, is verifiably the kimjammer clone's — its README states "I'm using MiniCPM-Llama3-V-2_5-int4" — and no VedalAI artifact names any vision model at all. The widely-repeated claim that vision and speech "cannot operate simultaneously" is NOT in the Wikipedia article it is cited to (verified by direct fetch); it traces to Wu & Lingel (2025) in New Media & Society, which is paywalled and reachable here only through a search-engine paraphrase. Unknown and should be reported as unknown: the vision model/provider, in-house vs API, resolution and polling rate, whether any webcam/real-world input exists, how reaction-video "watching" is actually implemented, and what the 2026 rewrite changed about any of it.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction #9 (vision concurrency): RESOLVED in favour of the 'watching' side, with the concurrency claim downgraded. The 'vision and speech cannot operate simultaneously' wording comes from Wu & Lingel (2025), an observational media-studies paper (paywalled, T4), not from Wikipedia as the claim set states, and not from Vedal. Vedal's own T1 explanation for exactly the failure mode the paper describes is different in kind: vision is "kind of stunted right now for the sake of latency" — a deliberate fidelity cap, not a scheduler-level mutual exclusion. Both observations are therefore compatible: Neuro can and does react to video (react content since Feb 2023, Kotaku via Wikipedia), but her image understanding is coarse enough to call a reindeer a dog. The report should say 'low-fidelity, latency-capped vision' and cite the paper only as an outside observation.
- Contradiction #10 (game-vision misattribution): RESOLVED in favour of angle 07's reading. The 80x60 monochannel/grayscale figure belongs to the osu!-playing game model specifically — namu.wiki states it as 'the game model that plays [osu!] is written in Python and is a monochannel 80x60 pixel size' and gives the RGB-training-cost rationale, and neurosama.fandom and virtualyoutuber.fandom corroborate it in osu!/game-AI framing. It must NOT be generalised to Minecraft or to general image vision: the official neuro-sdk README (T2) shows SDK-integrated games receive text game-state, and neuro-amongus describes structured game data rather than pixels, so Neuro's non-osu! game input is text, not a downsampled screen. Angle 06's call to retract the figure as 'unsourced' is too strong — it is real but wiki-tier (T5) and scoped to osu!.
- Clone-contamination flag #10 (vision section at risk of borrowing MiniCPM): RESOLVED and confirmed as a live risk. The kimjammer/Neuro repository itself names MiniCPM-Llama3-V-2_5-int4 as its own vision model and self-describes as a recreation, so the attribution is settled; and during this pass a WebSearch summary was observed asserting that 'Vedal uses MiniCPM-Llama3-V-2_5-int4 on a custom Neuro-LLM-Server'. The report must state that Neuro's vision model is publicly unnamed and call out MiniCPM by name as clone-derived.
- Clone-contamination flag #8 (2B / q2_k lore): RESOLVED as unverifiable and probably clone-derived. Two separate claims in this cluster (01-vedal-primary#07, 09-timeline-2026#21) assert the figure has no primary source; I confirmed that it appears only as an uncited sentence on virtualyoutuber.fandom and is entirely absent from Wikipedia's raw wikitext. Combined with the fact that 'q2_k' is a llama.cpp/GGUF label from hobbyist local-LLM practice, it should be presented as community lore with a plausible clone-culture origin, not as Neuro's model size.
- #9 Vision concurrency (angle 11 'vision and speech cannot run simultaneously' vs angle 06 'watches video and reacts live'): RESOLVED as a sourcing artefact. The simultaneity claim is not in the Wikipedia page it is cited to (verified by direct fetch) and traces to Wu & Lingel 2025, which is paywalled and only paraphrased by a search engine; the 'watching video' claim is a content-format observation (documented since Feb 2023), not an architectural statement. They are not actually in contradiction — one is an unverified claim about scheduling, the other about programming format. Report the latency-capped vision design (T1, Vedal, Sep 2025) instead of either.
- #10 Game-vision misattribution (80x60 grayscale): RESOLVED in favour of the brief's hypothesis. Three wikis attach 80x60 monochrome Python input to the game-playing AI (osu!/Minecraft), the official SDK path is text-only (verified T2), and nothing ties 80x60 to the captcha/reaction-video vision. The figure must be reported as the gameplay agent's input at wiki tier, never as 'how Neuro sees'.
- Clone flag #10 (MiniCPM-Llama3-V is the only named vision model in the claim set): RESOLVED as confirmed contamination. kimjammer/Neuro's own README names 'MiniCPM-Llama3-V-2_5-int4'; no VedalAI artifact names any vision model. The vision section must state 'model unknown' rather than borrowing it.
- Clone flag #8 ('~2B params, q2_k'): RESOLVED as unsourced. No primary source located; additionally, the T1-confirmed May 2026 hardware (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q, 128 GB DDR5 ECC, Threadripper PRO 9000WX) is an affirmative argument against a 2-bit-quantised 2B deployment.
- Captcha-date split (22 Jul 2024 vs 29 Sep 2025), flagged as unresolved in r2-08: RESOLVED to 29 Sep 2025 via the official channel's VOD title plus the clip channel's 'last dev stream' note (uploaded 7 Oct 2025). The 22 Jul 2024 dev stream is a separate event whose fan-wiki recap discusses vision latency but never a captcha — the two got merged in community retellings.
