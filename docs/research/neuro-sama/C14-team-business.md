## Summary A (evidence hunter)
Only two things in this cluster are established at primary-source strength. First, vedal.ai is Vedal's own site and its text is reliable T2: the footer reads "© 2026 Vedal AI", contact is vedal@vedal.ai, it names neurosama.shop and the Taobao store "Neurosama中国商城" as the only official merch stores, it advertises a live "Neuro & Evil" concert on 19 December 2026 at The Vermont Hollywood in Los Angeles, and it carries no careers or jobs section (/careers/ 404s). Second, "Vedal AI" is a brand, not a verified company: a direct UK Companies House search returns no entity named Vedal AI or Neurosama, and NEURO SWISS AI LTD (#16302608) has no evidenced connection to Vedal — so the finder's cautious wording survives verification. Two claims needed correcting. The merch claim's quoted sentence is exact, but "sold exclusively" is wrong: Vedal himself promoted Makeship plushie campaigns on X (T1, June 2023), Makeship hosts a "Vedal" creator shop, and neurosama.shop is itself a Juniper Creates-run storefront. The "no talent agency" claim is disputed rather than supported: Mythic Talent's own 2026 creators roster omits Neuro-sama and Vedal, but the Neuro-sama Fandom, Virtual YouTuber Wiki and HoloList all list Mythic Talent as her affiliation, and a clip titled "Vedal talks about joining Mythic" exists — the honest statement is that Neuro is not under a Hololive/Nijisanji-style IP-owning agency, while third-party management representation is unresolved. The VTuber Awards wins are real and correctly dated (16 Dec 2023, 14 Dec 2024) but only wiki-cited (T4) and credited to Vedal987 rather than Neuro-sama, and the awards are produced by Mythic Talent — the same company whose affiliation status is in question. What remains unknown for this cluster: whether any legal entity exists in any jurisdiction, whether a management contract exists, the identities and pay status of the volunteer team (Chrchie / Ellie / J0ker_jd / "Alex"), and any Vedal-disclosed revenue or cost figures — every income number in circulation is an outside estimate.

- Contradiction #11 (Twitch subscriber figures): resolved as one figure plus restatements, not three measurements. The only well-dated number is Dexerto's 162,459 active paid subscribers as of 2026-01-02 (angle 12); angle 08's 'over 160,000 active subscribers by January 2026' is a rounding of that same snapshot. The '165,268 paid subs' figure in an undated ~Sep 2026 financial post carries no independent date or methodology and should be presented as an undated restatement of the same early-2026 peak, not as a September 2026 measurement. Separately, Wikipedia's 343,215 'total subscribers' on 2026-01-05 is a DIFFERENT metric (cumulative subs during the third subathon's hype trains, which peaked at level 126), not active paid subs, and must not be compared against the 162k figure. Distractify's '200,000+ paid subscribers / 304,048 followers' is an undated third snapshot and is internally inconsistent with its own follower count, so it should be dropped rather than averaged in.
- Clone-contamination flag #11, first half (angle 12's 'No confirmed cloud provider or hardware for Neuro's real backend was found' carrying a [high/clone-project] source grade): resolved and retired. That finding existed only because angle 12 could not fetch YouTube transcripts, so the only hardware specs it saw were kimjammer/Neuro's clone stack (LLAMA 3 8B EXL2, oobabooga, 12GB VRAM).
