# C16-rewrite-roadmap — 2026 codebase rewrite & roadmap
Verdict counts: {'confirmed': 4, 'unverifiable': 1, 'disputed': 2, 'refuted': 2, 'likely': 1}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
The 2026 rewrite is established at T1 from Vedal's own dev streams, and the cluster's original framing badly undersells it. On 29 March 2026 Vedal announced he was "rewriting the whole thing from the ground up... pretty much all the systems," because code dating to 2019-2020 was fatally coupled to Unity and leaking memory; on 28 April 2026 the rewrite became the production system ("every stream going forward now should be on the rewrite"), leaving only redeems and karaoke duets unimplemented, with the new architecture letting Neuro and Evil "survive outside of Unity" (Unity retained for rendering only) and delivering a >100 FPS leak-free VR stream. The widely repeated "June 6, Vedal announced plans to rewrite half of Neuro's codebase" sentence is refuted: it is a search-engine AI synthesis, a Wikipedia insource search for the phrase returns zero hits site-wide, and both its date and its "half" scope contradict Vedal's own words. The hardware half of the roadmap is equally solid at T1 — a self-built server (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q ~$10k, Threadripper PRO 9965WX 24c/48t, 128 GB DDR5 ECC, 28 TB, dual 1200 W PSUs into a UPS, ProxMox, wheeled rack) blogged 25 May 2026 and built on stream — and the previously open "did it ever go live?" question is now closed as of 18 July 2026, when Vedal told Evil on an official-channel dev stream "today you are running on your new server," said he had "switched to Linux" and ported everything including his custom OBS plugins, and noted some components were still on the old machine pending migration. The single biggest roadmap fact missing from the claim list is from that same 18 July 2026 stream: Vedal is moving Neuro and Evil onto reasoning models ("there's a new paradigm shift... they became reasoning models... they can sort of wait before they respond, think about what it is, and then actually do it"), developed on the new server, hot-swappable with the old model as fallback, with repetition and voice change named as the risks. Two supporting claims survive as verified negatives: English Wikipedia (fetched 2026-09-22) documents none of this, and the public VedalAI GitHub org shows only routine per-game SDK activity through 21 August 2026 — exactly what you would expect if the rewrite lives in the private core. What remains unknown: what model family or size the reasoning-model upgrade actually uses, whether the memory subsystem was redesigned by the rewrite, how much of Neuro (as opposed to Evil) had migrated to the new server after July, and whether the "idol agency / more AI streamers" ambition is anything more than uncited Fandom prose.

## Summary B (skeptic)
What is solidly established for this cluster is the rewrite itself, at T1, from Vedal's own words on his own dev streams: the pre-rewrite code dated to 2019–2020, the rewrite was driven mainly by years of unfixable Unity memory leaks, it decouples Neuro's and Evil's core from Unity ("Neuro and Evil are able to survive outside of Unity"), it was described on 29 Mar 2026 as covering "pretty much all the systems", and it went into production on 28 Apr 2026 with redeems and karaoke duets still unimplemented. The May 2026 hardware upgrade is equally well sourced: the official blog post of 25 May 2026 (28 TB storage, multiple PSUs, dual UPS, ProxMox, an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q at ~$10k) plus Vedal's 18/25 May build streams (Threadripper PRO 9000WX-series 24c/48t, 128 GB DDR5 ECC, ASUS "Sage" workstation board, dual 1200W PSUs, wheeled rack, GPU shared between Neuro and Evil). The 19 Dec 2026 Vermont Hollywood concert is confirmed at T2 by the official concert page and Pollstar, and the 21 Sep 2026 "big announcement" turns out to have been that concert, not a technical roadmap item. Three widely repeated items should be dropped or heavily caveated: the "June 6, announced plans to rewrite half of Neuro's codebase" line is a search-engine synthesis with no primary page, is dated by search engines to 2023, and contradicts Vedal's own 2026 "all the systems" framing; the "idol agency / more AI streamers / mommy AI VTuber" roadmap is uncited fan-wiki text with no Vedal statement behind it; and the "within-session context only, no cross-session memory" characterisation is refuted by Vedal's June 2025 description of long-term memory upgrades, with its RAG hedge traceable to clone projects. What remains genuinely unknown is the technical substance of the rewrite — no T1/T2 source says what changed in the model, memory design, or inference stack — and whether Neuro's live inference ever moved onto the new server: it was explicitly still being built on 25 May 2026, reportedly not yet in use ~14 Jun 2026, with a ~18 Jul 2026 cutover appearing only in T5 search synthesis. Corroborating routes are also closed: English Wikipedia (re-checked 2026-09-22) documents no 2026 rewrite or hardware at all, the Neuro-sama Fandom wiki has no 2025/2026 dev-stream articles, and the public neuro-sdk changelog shows only additive 2026 changes — which is weak evidence about the private core either way. One tangential datapoint cuts against a "total" rewrite: a wiki page citing a Discord source says the osu!-playing bot was still on "an old codebase" as of 27 Jul 2026.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction 3 (memory: offline fine-tuning vs. runtime memory system): RESOLVED in favour of a runtime memory component. Vedal's own June 2025 dev stream describes building and shipping "changes to the long-term and short-term memory" designed so that "you shouldn't forget anything that happens in a stream" and that long-term "you should be better at remembering important things and keeping that information for longer", with "lower latency" as a side benefit, and says the only way to test it is "by actually streaming with it... what happens over multiple streams". A curated-offline-fine-tuning-only explanation cannot produce a system that is tested stream-to-stream and ships with a latency figure. This also refutes claim r2-01#08's "within-session context only".
- Clone-contamination flag 2 (RAG / Chroma / FAISS / MemGPT cross-session retrieval): RESOLVED as clone-derived. The only textual basis in this cluster is the lin-guanguo page's hedge "some indications of retrieval-based memory (RAG-like) for cross-session facts, but not publicly documented" — which I read directly and which cites nothing. No Vedal statement names any vector store or retrieval framework. The report should say Vedal built a bespoke long-term/short-term memory system of undisclosed design, and must not name RAG, Chroma, FAISS or MemGPT.
- Clone-contamination flag 8 (~2B parameters / q2_k): RESOLVED as unverifiable and probably clone-back-formed. I confirmed verbatim that the April 2026 community audit traces both figures to Fandom wikis only. Independently, the July 2026 T1 evidence makes the lore actively implausible for the current system: Vedal is running a reasoning-model class LLM on a 96 GB RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, which is not a 2B q2_k hobbyist GGUF deployment. Any report statement of "2B / q2_k" must be labelled unverified pre-2026 community lore, not architecture.
- Contradiction 1 (turn latency 700ms vs 1-3s vs "low latency"): PARTIALLY RESOLVED for this cluster only. The 1-3 s figures on the lin-guanguo page are explicitly labelled there as clone/community project measurements (AIRIS-VtuberAI, kimjammer) and must not be attributed to Neuro; Vedal's own numbers are deltas, not absolutes (a ~400 ms increase traded for more "thinking" in Apr 2024). The March 2026 rewrite promised "lower latency" and the July 2026 reasoning model explicitly costs latency ("they do take a while sometimes cuz they're a bit more complicated"), so any single 2026 latency number in the report is unsupported.
- Rewrite-date confusion (the "June 6" claim vs. the clip-title timeline): RESOLVED. There is no June 6 rewrite announcement in any locatable source; a Wikipedia insource search for "rewrite half of Neuro's codebase" returns zero hits across the entire encyclopedia. The primary timeline is 29 Mar 2026 (plan, scope = "pretty much all the systems"), ~Apr 2026 (Evil switched to the new codebase in production testing), 28 Apr 2026 (official transition, "mostly done"), 27 Jul 2026 (osu! bot still on the old codebase). Any "half the codebase" figure should be dropped.

- contradictions.md clone-flag #2 (RAG / vector-DB memory is clone-derived) applied to r2-01#08: the 'some indications of retrieval-based memory (RAG-like) for cross-session facts' line must be dropped as an echo of Open-LLM-VTuber/MemGPT/Chroma clone design, not evidence about Neuro. Separately, the same claim's 'within-session context only' half is refuted by Vedal's own June 2025 long-term-memory statements, so the correct position is: a cross-session memory component exists (T1), mechanism unknown (no tier).
- contradictions.md #3 (memory: 'manually-curated offline fine-tuning only' vs dated, failing memory upgrades) resolved in favour of a runtime memory component: Vedal's June 2025 dev stream describes live-testing short- and long-term memory changes explicitly aimed at retention 'over multiple streams', and wiki sources record him stating Evil Neuro's long-term memory held three items (~Nov 2024).
