# C01-llm — LLM identity, training, personality
Verdict counts: {'confirmed': 7, 'disputed': 3, 'unverifiable': 1, 'likely': 3}

## Summary A (evidence hunter)
At the top tier (T1, Vedal's own words) only three things about Neuro's "brain" are actually established: that "the main technology behind Neuro-sama is a large language model (LLM)" (vedal.ai/advice, 2023), that Vedal deliberately refuses to explain his build because "I like my job right now, which is Neuro-sama" (same page, the only reason he has ever given for keeping it closed), and — from the Neuro Game SDK docs that Vedal987 authors himself (SPECIFICATION.md and BEST_PRACTICES.md, commits 20–21 Aug 2026) — a set of behavioural facts that only make sense for an LLM agent: action names, descriptions, schemas, state and query strings are "directly received by Neuro"; her emitted action `data` "comes directly from Neuro, so there is a chance it might be malformed, contain invalid JSON, or not match the provided schema exactly"; she "is much better at reading clearly stated information than at inferring"; and she "does not react well to being spammed with many small messages". The official README adds (T2) that high-APM games need a separate non-LLM system for low-level control. Dev-stream transcripts add T1 context that the LLM is the piece Vedal is least willing to promise anything about — in March 2026 he said "it's always a little bit harder to predict… especially when it comes to the language model stuff. I don't want to hype up stuff around that until I have my [stuff] in order" — and that he treats response latency as a direct trade against reasoning depth ("if she's doing more thinking to be more intelligent… it's going to take her longer", April 2024, a +400 ms regression accepted for an intelligence upgrade). Everything else in this cluster is weaker than the finders graded it: the popular "Vedal trained the LLM himself on his own Twitch interactions" line traces to a single uncited third-party Threads post, while the one named-outlet Vedal interview (Vice, 2023-01-04) says only that Neuro "was made using a large language model… trained from massive amounts of text taken from the open internet" — i.e. built on a pretrained model; and the "offline batch fine-tuning only, no online learning" architecture is one community researcher's uncited synthesis, not a disclosure. Model identity, parameter count, quantization, base family, context length and fine-tuning method remain unknown at every tier — the widely repeated "2 billion parameters / q2_k" figure has no confirmed citation even on the wiki that hosts it, and NamuWiki's "presumably GPT-2" is explicitly labelled a guess by its own editors. Date discipline matters here: every architectural statement above the T5 line describes the system as of Aug 2026 SDK docs or earlier, and the Apr 2026 production rewrite plus a July 2026 clip showing Vedal A/B-testing Evil's "old model" against a "new reasoning model" mean the 2025-era lore is very likely stale.

## Summary B (skeptic)
Only three things in this cluster reach T1/T2. First, Vedal has said publicly exactly one substantive thing about the brain — "the main technology behind Neuro-sama is a large language model (LLM)" on vedal.ai/advice — and in the same sentence recommends OpenAI to readers who want to experiment, which is advice to hobbyists and not a disclosure of Neuro's stack; he refuses a build tutorial "because I like my job right now, which is Neuro-sama." Second, in a named-outlet interview (Kotaku, 6 Jan 2023) he said the LLM was "trained on a large amount of text on the internet" and that "data that she learns on is also manually curated to mitigate negative biases" — the only on-record evidence that a training-data pipeline under his control exists, and the only stated motive for it is bias mitigation, not capability. Third, the official neuro-sdk (verified live today) fixes the interface shape: plaintext game state and instructions "will be directly received by Neuro" (four occurrences), her action parameters "come directly from Neuro" and "might be malformed, contain invalid JSON, or not match the provided schema," she is "much better at reading clearly stated information than at inferring," and high-APM games need a separate non-LLM system for low-level control — a text-in/text-out LLM agent with no output-schema guarantee, and nothing about which model. Everything else in this cluster is T5 or worse: model identity, parameter count, quantization, base family, fine-tuning method, and whether personality lives in the weights or the system prompt are all unverifiable. The "~2B parameters / q2_k" figure has a single origin (an unfootnoted Fandom Trivia bullet) that the compiling researcher's own April-2026 audit traced back to Fandom only, q2_k is a llama.cpp/GGUF label typical of hobbyist local setups, and it sits oddly with the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell / 128 GB DDR5 ECC server Vedal built on stream in May 2026. The "Vedal trained the LLM himself on his own Twitch interactions with express permission" story traces to one 2025 Threads post by a third party, and the "deploy→collect→retrain, no online learning" model rests on one community page that cites nothing for it; the clone-derived supporting argument ("prompting can't make an aligned model swear, so Neuro must be fine-tuned") is refuted at source, since kimjammer's own devlog concludes prompting sufficed and he deliberately avoided LoRA. Finally, every characterization above describes at best the pre-April-2026 system: Vedal said on his 29 Mar 2026 dev stream that "the language model stuff" was the hardest part of the rewrite to predict and that he would not discuss it "until I have my [stuff] in order," and no source of any tier states what the post-rewrite brain is.

## Contradictions resolved
- Contradiction 4 (personality in weights vs system prompt): RESOLVED IN FAVOUR OF 'UNKNOWN'. The 'personality baked into fine-tuned weights, prompts only for situational context' formulation traces to lin-guanguo's page, which my fetch confirms carries no citation for it, and the competing Hugging Face-forum view ('a system prompt acting as a core personality anchor') is equally uncited. Neither side has a Vedal statement. The report must say the weights-vs-prompt split is undocumented, and must drop the medium-confidence grade that angles 03 and 09 assigned it.
- Contradiction 3 (memory: offline fine-tuning vs runtime memory component): RESOLVED IN FAVOUR OF A RUNTIME COMPONENT EXISTING. The fine-tuning-only explanation is T5 and uncited, whereas Vedal-primary dev-stream audio (10 Jun 2025) describes shipping "changes to the long-term and short-term memory" as a discrete, separately-testable subsystem with its own latency profile ("this system comes with lower latency") that he had "never tried before". Discrete, dated, independently-failing memory upgrades are incompatible with 'apparent memory is only a side-effect of fine-tuning'. Both a runtime memory system and offline data curation can be true; only the former has primary evidence.
- Contradiction 12 (early history: 2018/2019 osu! bot, 2021 'Airis' prototype, 'GPT-2/GPT-3-era 2021 prototype'): PARTLY RESOLVED. Wiki sources consistently state Vedal built an osu!-playing AI in 2018–2019, demonstrated a separate Live2D AI VTuber prototype named Airis on 5 Aug 2021, and merged the two around March 2022, relaunching as VTuber Neuro-sama on 19 Dec 2022 — so 'Airis' and 'the 2021 prototype' are the same system, and it IS the conversational ancestor of the Dec 2022 relaunch. What remains unresolved and must NOT be asserted is which language model that prototype used; the 'GPT-2/GPT-3-era' attribution is clone-author speculation (kimjammer) plus NamuWiki guesswork, at T5 with explicit hedging.
- Clone flag 8 ('~2B parameters, q2_k'): UPHELD AND STRENGTHENED. No claim in this cluster asserts it, and it should stay out of any 'what we know' section. Its only host is one Fandom page with no confirmed footnote on that sentence; lin-guanguo's April 2026 audit traces it to Fandom wikis only; and q2_k is a llama.cpp/GGUF label typical of hobbyist local-LLM builds, so it is plausibly back-formed from clone culture. Report it, if at all, as lore whose provenance failed verification — not as an unverified-but-plausible spec.
- Clone flag 12 (kimjammer's swearing experiment used as support for Neuro being fine-tuned): UPHELD, AND THE UNDERLYING FINDING IS ALSO MISQUOTED. Fetching devlog #4 (2024-01-03) shows kimjammer reported the aligned model "stubbornly avoids swearing" in natural conversation but would swear when asked directly, and that he was "reassured that I wouldn't have to train a whole LORA on getting a specific style" — he chose prompting over fine-tuning. So this is not merely 'an argument, not a fact' about Neuro; as commonly restated ('only fine-tuning overrides alignment at the weight level') it also misrepresents the clone experiment it comes from.
- Clone flag 7 ('Govorunb/gary' described as a backend implementation for the Neuro SDK): UPHELD as it touches this cluster's LLM-identity question. Gary is a local-LLM test simulator that stands in for Neuro so SDK developers can test integrations; nothing about its model choice says anything about Neuro's brain. Any claim of the form 'Neuro's backend uses <model X>' sourced from gary or Jippity is clone/tooling contamination.
- Clone flag 11 (claims about Neuro carrying clone-project source grades): UPHELD for the LLM cluster. The '2021 prototype used GPT-2/GPT-3-era technology' line is kimjammer's own speculation about Vedal and must be re-derived or dropped, not carried as a fact about Neuro. Likewise, the frequently repeated 'Vedal runs at least some of the AI in the cloud' is the same author's explicit 'presumably' guess, and is now superseded by T1 evidence of the May 2026 locally-built server.
- Contradiction 1 (latency figures 1–3 s vs 700 ms vs 'low'): PARTLY RESOLVED for the LLM angle. The '1–3 s' figure in the community research page is explicitly stated there to be clone/community-project latency (AIRIS on an RTX 4080, kimjammer with DeepSpeed), not Neuro's, so it should never have been carried as Neuro's number. Vedal-primary evidence gives only relative movements: a ~400 ms regression traded for intelligence (Apr 2024) and a 900 ms–1 s to ~700 ms reduction relayed by the 22 Jul 2024 dev-stream wiki page. Neuro's absolute end-to-end latency has never been disclosed at T1.
- Contradiction 4 (personality in weights vs system prompt) — RESOLVED AS UNKNOWN, NOT MEDIUM. The 'baked into weights from v3 onward, prompts only for situational context' framing traces to a single community page (lin-guanguo) which, when queried directly, cites no primary source for it; the opposing 'system prompt as personality anchor' view is Hugging Face forum reasoning, equally unsourced. Vedal's only on-record statements (vedal.ai/advice; Kotaku 6 Jan 2023) establish that an LLM is used and that he curates training data, and say nothing about where personality lives. Angles 03 and 09 should be downgraded from medium to unverifiable, exactly as angle 10 argued.
- Contradiction 6 ('V3' label collision) — RESOLVED. No 'V3' in any source refers to an LLM version. Per NamuWiki's avatar tables, Neuro's V1/V2/V3 are Live2D avatars (2022-12-19 / 2023-05-27 / 2024-12-19) and Evil's are 2023-03-25 / 2023-06-25 / 2025-03-25 — so the 'V3 model, March 25 2025' floating in the claim set is Evil Neuro's V3 AVATAR, not a brain upgrade. Separately, 'V3 singing plugin' (neurosama.fandom Karaoke page, dated by NamuWiki to June 2023) and 'V3 voice' (the TTS effort Vedal described in June 2025 as still unshipped) are two further independent version tracks. Four unrelated numbering schemes; none is an LLM version, and none should be reported as one.
- Contradiction 12 (early history: 2018 osu! bot / 2021 'Airis' / '2021 prototype used GPT-2/GPT-3-era tech') — PARTLY RESOLVED. Airis is real and well-dated across three wikis: a concept document plus a private YouTube demo on 5 Aug 2021 using Live2D's default Hiyori Momose model, merged with the 2018-19 osu! bot around March 2022 to become the December 2022 Neuro-sama. Airis and 'the 2021 prototype' are therefore the same system, and it IS an ancestor of the Dec 2022 relaunch. But the 'GPT-2/GPT-3-era' characterization must be dropped: NamuWiki's Korean footnote 6 explicitly labels it inference from the debut date ('it is highly likely that a local LLM was developed using GPT-2'), and kimjammer's version is a clone author's speculation about someone else's system. No source at any tier names Airis's or Neuro's base model.
- Clone-contamination flag 8 ('~2B parameters, q2_k') — RESOLVED AS UNVERIFIABLE AND PLAUSIBLY CLONE-CULTURE BACK-FORMATION. The sole origin is a single Trivia>System bullet on virtualyoutuber.fandom with no footnote confirmed adjacent to it; lin-guanguo's own web-search audit (April 2026) found the numbers 'trace back to Fandom Wikis only'. q2_k is a llama.cpp/GGUF quantization label idiomatic to hobbyist local-inference setups, and a 2-bit GGUF quant is an odd fit for the machine Vedal actually built on stream in May 2026 (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q, 128 GB DDR5 ECC, Threadripper PRO). Report as unverified lore explicitly, never as a spec.
- Clone-contamination flag 12 / claim 10-community-academic#04 — RESOLVED AGAINST THE CLAIM, at source. I fetched kimjammer's dev log 4 (3 Jan 2024) twice: his conclusions are 'Good characterization is possible with the system prompt' and 'I was reassured that I wouldn't have to train a whole LORA on getting a specific style - the LLM could just handle my request.' The only observation matching the claim is that the aligned model 'stubbornly avoids swearing... won't generate swears in conversation naturally' — and the sentence quoted as his in the claim set ('aligned base models stubbornly resist certain behaviors... even when explicitly prompted') is not his wording but lin-guanguo's gloss. This argument must be removed from the report's support for Neuro being fine-tuned; it points the other way, and it is a clone observation regardless.
- Clone-contamination flag 3 (32k context window) — RESOLVED. The figure is verified in kimjammer's own devlog as his config: 'the whole system prompt is around 1k tokens, which isn't too bad for a model with 32k context length.' It is his clone's number. The only Neuro-side statement about context is Vedal's June 2025 'longer context length' with no figure attached. No context-window number for Neuro exists at any tier.
