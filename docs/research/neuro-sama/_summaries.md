## C01-llm — LLM identity, training, personality
- A: At the top tier (T1, Vedal's own words) only three things about Neuro's "brain" are actually established: that "the main technology behind Neuro-sama is a large language model (LLM)" (vedal.ai/advice, 2023), that Vedal deliberately refuses to explain his build because "I like my job right now, which is Neuro-sama" (same page, the only reason he has ever given for keeping it closed), and — from the Neuro Game SDK docs that Vedal987 authors himself (SPECIFICATION.md and BEST_PRACTICES.md, commits 20–21 Aug 2026) — a set of behavioural facts that only make sense for an LLM agent: action names, descriptions, schemas, state and query strings are "directly received by Neuro"; her emitted action `data` "comes directly from Neuro, so there is a chance it might be malformed, contain invalid JSON, or not match the provided schema exactly"; she "is much better at reading clearly stated information than at inferring"; and she "does not react well to being spammed with many small messages". The official README adds (T2) that high-APM games need a separate non-LLM system for low-level control. Dev-stream transcripts add T1 context that the LLM is the piece Vedal is least willing to promise anything about — in March 2026 he said "it's always a little bit harder to predict… especially when it comes to the language model stuff. I don't want to hype up stuff around that until I have my [stuff] in order" — and that he treats response latency as a direct trade against reasoning depth ("if she's doing more thinking to be more intelligent… it's going to take her longer", April 2024, a +400 ms regression accepted for an intelligence upgrade). Everything else in this cluster is weaker than the finders graded it: the popular "Vedal trained the LLM himself on his own Twitch interactions" line traces to a single uncited third-party Threads post, while the one named-outlet Vedal interview (Vice, 2023-01-04) says only that Neuro "was made using a large language model… trained from massive amounts of text taken from the open internet" — i.e. built on a pretrained model; and the "offline batch fine-tuning only, no online learning" architecture is one community researcher's uncited synthesis, not a disclosure. Model identity, parameter count, quantization, base family, context length and fine-tuning method remain unknown at every tier — the widely repeated "2 billion parameters / q2_k" figure has no confirmed citation even on the wiki that hosts it, and NamuWiki's "presumably GPT-2" is explicitly labelled a guess by its own editors. Date discipline matters here: every architectural statement above the T5 line describes the system as of Aug 2026 SDK docs or earlier, and the Apr 2026 production rewrite plus a July 2026 clip showing Vedal A/B-testing Evil's "old model" against a "new reasoning model" mean the 2025-era lore is very likely stale.
- B: Only three things in this cluster reach T1/T2. First, Vedal has said publicly exactly one substantive thing about the brain — "the main technology behind Neuro-sama is a large language model (LLM)" on vedal.ai/advice — and in the same sentence recommends OpenAI to readers who want to experiment, which is advice to hobbyists and not a disclosure of Neuro's stack; he refuses a build tutorial "because I like my job right now, which is Neuro-sama." Second, in a named-outlet interview (Kotaku, 6 Jan 2023) he said the LLM was "trained on a large amount of text on the internet" and that "data that she learns on is also manually curated to mitigate negative biases" — the only on-record evidence that a training-data pipeline under his control exists, and the only stated motive for it is bias mitigation, not capability. Third, the official neuro-sdk (verified live today) fixes the interface shape: plaintext game state and instructions "will be directly received by Neuro" (four occurrences), her action parameters "come directly from Neuro" and "might be malformed, contain invalid JSON, or not match the provided schema," she is "much better at reading clearly stated information than at inferring," and high-APM games need a separate non-LLM system for low-level control — a text-in/text-out LLM agent with no output-schema guarantee, and nothing about which model. Everything else in this cluster is T5 or worse: model identity, parameter count, quantization, base family, fine-tuning method, and whether personality lives in the weights or the system prompt are all unverifiable. The "~2B parameters / q2_k" figure has a single origin (an unfootnoted Fandom Trivia bullet) that the compiling researcher's own April-2026 audit traced back to Fandom only, q2_k is a llama.cpp/GGUF label typical of hobbyist local setups, and it sits oddly with the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell / 128 GB DDR5 ECC server Vedal built on stream in May 2026. The "Vedal trained the LLM himself on his own Twitch interactions with express permission" story traces to one 2025 Threads post by a third party, and the "deploy→collect→retrain, no online learning" model rests on one community page that cites nothing for it; the clone-derived supporting argument ("prompting can't make an aligned model swear, so Neuro must be fine-tuned") is refuted at source, since kimjammer's own devlog concludes prompting sufficed and he deliberately avoided LoRA. The official Neuro-sama blog of 25 May 2026 independently supplies 28 TB of storage, "dual-UPS", ProxMox as hypervisor, and the GPU's ~$10,000 price — so storage, hypervisor and price are T2, while CPU, RAM, motherboard class, PSU/UPS topology and GPU model are T1. Self-hosting is therefore settled: Neuro and Evil run on hardware Vedal owns and assembled by hand, no cloud provider has ever been named, and the older clone-blog speculation that "he is in fact running at least some of the AI in the cloud, presumably with a provider" is refuted clone-derived guesswork from January 2024. Two hardware-adjacent facts are firm and useful: Twitch-event ingestion shared the AI machine badly enough that the January 2025 subathon drove CPU usage to 100 % (Sportskeeda, not Wikipedia, which carries no hardware content at all), and the lava-lamp / plasma-globe stream props were literally wired to the host machine's RAM usage as a crash warning before being retired after a hardware upgrade. - B: Neuro-sama's inference hardware is, uniquely among this project's topics, genuinely well-established at the top tier for one moment in time: on 18 and 25 May 2026 Vedal announced and then hand-built on camera a server for Neuro and Evil comprising a 24-core/48-thread Zen 5 Threadripper PRO (SKU heard as 9965WX), 128 GB DDR5 ECC RAM on an 8-slot ASUS "Sage"-class workstation board chosen for PCIe lanes, dual 1200 W PSUs (one redundant, staged for GPUs not yet installed) feeding a UPS in a wheeled rack, and at least one NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition Max-Q; the official blog independently adds 28 TB of storage, dual-UPS, ProxMox as hypervisor and the GPU's ~$10,000 price (T1/T2, verified by direct fetch and by verbatim stream transcript). That single GPU is the hard architectural constraint worth putting in the report: an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell carries 96 GB of GDDR7, and Vedal states on stream that Neuro and Evil share it, which bounds whatever LLM, TTS and vision models are co-resident. What is NOT established is deployment: the blog describes the machine as unfinished on 25 May 2026, the only claimed status checkpoint after that ("not on the new server yet, but working fine", ~14 June 2026) exists solely as a search-engine paraphrase of an unreadable video title, and every weekly blog post from June through 21 September 2026 — including the September posts the earlier finder wrongly reported as non-existent — is pure in-character narrative with zero infrastructure content, so cutover is formally unresolved as of this research date. Everything predating May 2026 is far weaker: "self-hosted on Vedal's own GPUs" before 2026 rests on an undated secondhand stream relay, no CPU/RAM/storage list has ever been published for whatever machine ran her in 2023–2025, and the November 2024 IRL build stream is date-confirmed by an archived Twitch VOD but its RTX 4090 was (per wiki only) defective and refunded and was never tied to production inference. Three items in this cluster should be struck rather than softened: the January 2025 "CPU hit 100%" incident, whose Wikipedia citation is false and which no other source carries; the 2021 GPT-2/cloud-provider story, which is clone-author speculation contradicted by the 2026 evidence; and the Grokipedia i9-10900K/RTX 4090/128 GB list, which is T6 AI-generated content with no source. ## C03-memory — Memory system
- A: The load-bearing conclusion this cluster inherited from the Lin-Guanguo community doc — "within-session context only, no confirmed persistent cross-session memory" — is refuted at T1 by Vedal's own words on the 9/10 June 2025 dev stream (youtube.com/watch?v=HbraxW11WSU): he describes shipping "changes to the long-term and short-term memory," states the goal that Neuro "shouldn't forget anything that happens in a stream," that "long-term you should be better at remembering important things and keeping that information for longer," that "this system comes with lower latency," and that what he is testing is "her ability to remember what happens in a stream, what happens over multiple streams." A discrete long-term memory system therefore exists and is explicitly cross-session by design; what remains genuinely undocumented is its implementation (no confirmed RAG, vector DB, summarizer, or consolidation step — the only official artifact that touches retention is the neuro-sdk spec's `ephemeral_context` flag, which states that non-ephemeral `state`/`query` context "will be remembered by Neuro after the actions force is completed"). The competing "memory is really just irregular offline fine-tuning" theory is refuted as an explanation of long-term memory: Vedal frames memory as a runtime subsystem with its own latency profile and never ties it to retraining, and NamuWiki reports him saying Evil Neuro's long-term memory held only three countable items around late 2024 with expansion planned — a small slot-based store, not an open-ended embedding index. Manual curation of training data is separately T1-confirmed, but from Vedal's January 2023 Kotaku interview ("Data that she learns on is also manually curated to mitigate negative biases"), i.e. it originated as a safety/bias fix, not a memory mechanism. Evidence for the dated upgrade events is weak and mostly negative-space: the "August 1 2024 short-term memory upgrade → Broken Record loops" story rests on one uncited wiki passage echoed by search-engine summaries, and the clip cited for it is dated May 2024; the June 2025 event is the only memory upgrade with a primary transcript. No verbatim, dated example of correct cross-session recall has ever been published, no end-of-stream consolidation process is described anywhere, catastrophic-forgetting handling is never discussed, and the effect of the April 2026 codebase rewrite (whose driver was Unity *memory leaks*, an unrelated sense of "memory") on the memory subsystem is unknown. Every RAG / vector-DB / MemGPT / Chroma-FAISS statement in this cluster traces to clone projects (AIRI's DuckDB-WASM + pglite "Memory Alaya", Open-LLM-VTuber, kimjammer) or to AI-generated aggregator text, never to Vedal.
- B: The load-bearing claim of this cluster — that Neuro-sama has "within-session context only, no confirmed persistent cross-session memory" — is refuted at T1. In the 9/10 June 2025 dev stream Vedal says verbatim: "changes to the long-term and short-term memory of you. So the idea is theoretically you shouldn't forget anything that happens in a stream more or less. And also long-term you should be better at remembering important things and keeping that information for longer. And this system comes with lower latency," and that what he is testing is "her ability to remember what happens in a stream, what happens over multiple streams." A discrete long-term memory *store* (not fine-tuned weights) is further evidenced at T4 by NamuWiki's footnote quoting Vedal that Evil Neuro "currently only has three long-term memories," one occupied by "AI wanting to come out into reality," with expansion planned (~Nov 2024), and by virtualyoutuber.fandom stating Evil received "her own voice, model, personality, and memory bank" — which also refutes the claim that no source addresses whether Evil shares Neuro's memory. What is genuinely established is therefore: a runtime, per-character, long-term + short-term memory subsystem exists and is shipped; it was small and slot-like in late 2024; it was rebuilt/extended in mid-2025 with lower latency; and Vedal himself calls it experimental and testable only by streaming. What remains unknown at every tier is the *mechanism*: no primary or official source names RAG, a vector database, summarisation, embeddings, slot count for Neuro herself, retrain cadence, catastrophic-forgetting handling, or any end-of-stream consolidation step — the "RAG 2.0 / vector databases" line circulating in search-engine AI summaries traces only to a Medium essay and to clone stacks (AIRI's DuckDB-WASM/pglite/"Memory Alaya", Open-LLM-VTuber's removed LTM/Mem0), never to Vedal. Two widely repeated colour items in this cluster are misattributed: the "inconsistency of her memory" and "calling him a mosquito / repeating insults months ago" quotes are not present on the Fandom pages cited (verified by direct page read today), and the "Vedal runs Neuro overnight ... helps train her" quote is not in the English Wikipedia article, which instead states "Neuro-sama does not stream 24/7." Finally, date discipline: the Mar–Jun 2026 rewrite and the May 2026 server are about Unity *RAM* memory leaks and hardware, not cognitive memory — Vedal never mentions the memory subsystem in any rewrite quote, and the September 2026 "big announcement" turned out to be the 19 Dec 2026 Los Angeles live concert, so nothing newer than June 2025 is publicly known about how Neuro's memory works.

## C04-tts — TTS main voice
- A: What is genuinely established about Neuro's voice stack is mostly what Vedal has said about the voice he has NOT shipped. At T1 (his own 10 June 2025 dev stream) and T2 (his official vedal.ai New Year Resolutions 2026 page), "V3 voice" is a speaking-TTS upgrade he has chased since about 2023, pursued along two tracks — pre-training TTS models from scratch starting around November/December 2024 with "decent results" by March 2025, plus roughly 100 manual fine-tuning runs on open- and closed-source models "with a bunch of different companies," including genetic-algorithm search over speaker embeddings — and as of mid-2025 he judged it "passable" but "not a big enough upgrade," while the 2026 resolutions page still lists "Create V3 voice????????????????????" as unmet. The hard constraint he states is identity preservation, not quality: "it's super easy to give Neuro a voice upgrade... what's super hard to do is to keep the same voice and have an upgrade," and he concedes it is "impossible to compete with the multi-million dollar training run text-to-speech models" — which is the strongest indirect support for the long-standing belief that the shipped main voice is a commercial third-party TTS. By contrast, the specific engine claim (Microsoft Azure voice "Ashley," pitched up 25%) is T5 only: it appears as uncited fan-wiki text on neurosama.fandom and virtualyoutuber.fandom, is absent from English Wikipedia entirely (verified 22 Sep 2026 in both rendered text and wikitext), and its main "independent" corroboration (the lin-guanguo research page) is an uncited copy of the same sentence that self-flags its neighbouring figures as unverified — so it should be reported as undated community lore, never verified for 2025–2026 and never attributed to Vedal. Neuro's STT engine is unknown at every tier: the official VedalAI neuro-sdk documents only a text-in/text-out game API with no audio component at all, Wikipedia never mentions speech-to-text, and every "Whisper" attribution traces to clone projects (kimjammer/Neuro's faster-whisper tiny.en), which must be excluded. Singing is described by three separate wiki pages as a subsystem outside the LLM that shares one audio output with chat-TTS — corroborated indirectly by Vedal listing "duets in karaoke" as its own outstanding item in the April 2026 rewrite — but its V1/V2/V3 labels, its interruption bug, and above all its engine are unsourced; the sole engine name ever floated, "Synthesizer V Studio Pro 2" on NamuWiki's song subpage, is a machine-translated line whose own dating is self-contradictory and is absent from the Korean original, so no singing engine is established. Latency, finally, is the one voice-adjacent quantity with dated numbers: +400 ms accepted as the price of an intelligence upgrade (Vedal, April 2024, T1), and 900 ms–1 s reduced to "mostly 700 ms or lower" for both Neuro and Evil (22 July 2024, wiki-relayed dev stream, T5); nothing dated after the April 2026 rewrite or the May 2026 server rebuild updates either figure, so all engine- and latency-level statements in this cluster should be presented as pre-rewrite.
- B: Almost nothing about Neuro's voice stack is established above fan-wiki tier, and the one piece of hard evidence in this cluster overturns the finders' own framing. At T1, Vedal's 10 June 2025 dev stream establishes that "V3 voice" is a genuine spoken-TTS project, not an avatar label: he pre-trained TTS models from scratch starting around November/December 2024 (usable results by ~March 2025), experimented with genetic algorithms over speaker embeddings, then performed roughly 100 manual fine-tuning runs across open- and closed-source models with partner companies — and still declined to ship it, calling it "honestly passable" but "not a big enough upgrade," with the hard constraint being to improve naturalness while keeping the same recognisable voice. No source found confirms V3 voice shipped by September 2026, so the current main voice is formally unknown. The widely repeated "Microsoft Azure TTS, voice 'Ashley', pitched up 25%" claim collapses on inspection: English Wikipedia (re-read 2026-09-22) names no vendor, voice, pitch or engine at all, and the apparently independent corroborations — the Neuro-sama Fandom "nutshell" section, the Virtual YouTuber wiki, and the lin-guanguo research page — are uncited and mutually downstream, giving one undated origin rather than four witnesses, with nothing tying it to 2025-2026. STT is worse: no source of any tier names an engine, the official neuro-sdk documentation contains no mention of audio whatsoever, and every appearance of Whisper/faster-whisper traces to the kimjammer/Neuro clone, as does the "TTS into VTube Studio over a virtual audio cable" description, which is additionally contradicted by Vedal's own account of a custom Unity renderer his core process survives independently of. Singing is the best-supported structural fact — multiple wikis agree it is a separate system from the chat/LLM path (first song "Blinding Lights", 4 Jan 2023), and Vedal indirectly corroborates this by listing karaoke duets as a distinct unimplemented work item in the April 2026 rewrite — but its V1/V2/V3 history is uncited, the wikis contradict each other on whether speech interrupts singing or singing pauses speech, and the only named singing engine anywhere ("Synthesizer V Studio Pro 2", one machine-translated NamuWiki subpage) is uncorroborated and absent from both NamuWiki main articles. What is solid at news tier is the production side rather than the technology: official 2026 releases use "synthetically produced vocals" with human songwriting, production and vocal production by QueenPB, feeding November 2026 EPs and a 19 December 2026 live concert. Open for a report: the current TTS engine, the STT engine, the singing synthesis engine, whether V3 voice ever shipped, and whether the March-June 2026 rewrite (which Vedal said targets "lower latency") altered any of them.

## C05-stt-audio — STT, turn-taking, collab audio, voice-chat channel
- A: Only two things in this cluster are established at primary-source tier, and both come from documents Vedal wrote himself. First, the decision loop is serial: in VedalAI/neuro-sdk discussion #58 (22 Feb 2025) Vedal states that "she can only process one thing at once" and that context arriving mid-decision "will be merged after she is finished making her decision" — his separate remark about two simultaneous action/forces being processed in parallel and merged is explicitly flagged "untested" design intent, so the two statements are not in conflict once you see that one describes thinking and the other describes system-level handling. Second, the in-game voice path is fully specified in API/VOICE_CHAT.md, a file committed by Vedal987 on 19 Aug 2026 (SHA 6b79780): the game must tap each remote player's stream before mixing, register speakers as {id: uint16, name: string} where "name is what speech-to-text attribution uses", and send 48 kHz mono Float32 little-endian PCM with a 4-byte header carrying the speaker id (20 ms frames recommended, up to 32 concurrent speakers) — so who-said-what is supplied by the game, never inferred by diarization, and STT runs server-side on Neuro's own backend ("server ──► STT (attributed) ──► Neuro"), with her TTS returned as PCM for the game to inject into its voice-transmit path or a virtual microphone. That same T1 document gives the only 2026-dated latency statement in this cluster — "Hearing → speech-to-text → decision → text-to-speech is a full cognitive cycle (seconds, not milliseconds)" — which sits above the best historical number, a wiki recap of the 22 July 2024 dev stream reporting average delay cut from ~900 ms–1 s to "mostly 700 ms or lower" for both twins (T4, VOD linked); the widely repeated "1–3 seconds" figure is an unmeasured community inference and should not be reported as measured. No source at any tier names the STT engine Neuro actually uses: Whisper/faster-whisper appears only in clone projects (kimjammer/Neuro via RealtimeSTT) and a live search pass in this session showed search summaries still feeding that clone stack back as if it were Neuro's, so the engine must be reported as unknown. Turn-taking is otherwise documented only at SDK level and is well dated: actions/force gained a four-level priority on 17 Dec 2025 where "critical" interrupts her speech outright, speech_finished with isFinal marks the true end of an utterance (20 Aug 2026), and the voice channel exposes voice/speaking for push-to-talk keying and voice/cancelled for discarding buffered audio when she is interrupted. Everything about collab audio outside games remains T5 or worse: the Feb 2023 "interruption bug fixed in two days" story (Miyune 6 Feb, Camila 8 Feb 2023) is uncited wiki narrative, the "short complete sentences so the model doesn't guess how much time it has" rationale traces to an uncited EOS-token fan theory on the Virtual YouTuber wiki rather than to Vedal, and nothing names how Discord collab-partner audio reaches his stack — the closest official evidence being VOICE_CHAT.md's "voice-only clients" section, which says the same protocol can drive "Discord-like voice interfaces" with no game attached.
- B: The only tier-1/2 evidence in this cluster is the official SDK material, and it is unusually strong because Vedal987 wrote it himself: API/VOICE_CHAT.md (single commit 6b79780, "Add voice chat support", 2026-08-19, corroborated by the CHANGELOG entry for the same date) specifies the voice side-channel as a second WebSocket carrying 48 kHz mono Float32 LE PCM, with per-speaker streams tapped before mixing, speakers registered as {id, name}, and an explicit audio-flow diagram "game VC (per-player streams) → server → STT (attributed) → Neuro" — so speaker attribution is supplied by the game's display name and server-side STT is confirmed, with no diarization anywhere in the spec. Turn-taking is likewise documented at T1/T2 only for the SDK loop: Vedal stated on 22 Feb 2025 (neuro-sdk discussion #58) that while she is deciding "she can only process one thing at once" and incoming context "will be merged after she is finished making her decision", while the CHANGELOG adds a force `priority` field (17 Dec 2025) whose "critical" level interrupts her speech mid-utterance, a `speech_finished`/`isFinal` turn-flow message (20 Aug 2026), a ~20 s server-side action/result timeout (21 Aug 2026) and a `voice/cancelled` event for interrupted speech. Everything else in this cluster is fan-wiki or community-inference: the ~700 ms average latency figure is a Neuro-sama Fandom recap of the 22 Jul 2024 dev stream (real page, real VOD link, garbled numbers, never checked against the VOD), the 6 Feb 2023 Miyune / 8 Feb 2023 Camila collab dates are corroborated by independent VOD uploads but the "interruption bug fixed in two days" framing and every architectural inference drawn from it are uncited wiki wording. No source, at any tier, names the STT engine Neuro actually uses — the only named engines (faster-whisper tiny.en via RealtimeSTT) come from the kimjammer/Neuro clone and must never be carried over — and no source describes how Vedal routes Discord collab audio into his stack; the closest official artifact is VOICE_CHAT.md's "Voice-only clients" section, which documents a voice-socket-only client "useful for building Discord-like voice interfaces", not a statement about what Vedal runs. The "short complete sentences in voice chat" design rationale is not a Vedal statement at all: it is a paraphrase of an uncited fan-theory passage about withheld EOS tokens in the Virtual YouTuber wiki's Trivia>System section. Date discipline matters here: every quantitative latency figure predates the Mar–Jun 2026 rewrite (which Vedal said explicitly targets "lower latency") and the May 2026 Threadripper/RTX PRO 6000 server, and Vedal's own doc warns the full hear→STT→decide→TTS cycle is "seconds, not milliseconds", so none of the 700 ms / 1–3 s numbers can be reported as current for 2026.

## C06-singing — Singing / music pipeline
- A: What is solidly established about Neuro's singing is architectural, not technological. Three mutually independent wikis agree that singing runs on a subsystem separate from the chat/LLM path — it "does not use the LLM model", the speaking TTS is paused while it plays, and the two paths could historically collide (Neuro thanking donors mid-song) — and Vedal himself corroborates the modularity at T1 by listing "duets in karaoke need to be done" as an unfinished item of the April 2026 codebase rewrite, i.e. karaoke is a discrete component that had to be re-implemented. No source at any tier names the singing engine: not Vedal, not VedalAI's repos, not Wikipedia, not either NamuWiki language edition, and the widely circulated "Synthesizer V" attribution could not be traced to any source in this pass (the "V1/V2/V3 singing plugin" nomenclature comes from a completely uncited Fandom page and should be reported as community lore, while NamuWiki separately dates a "V3 singing voice" to June 2023 — which is not the V3 Live2D avatar of December 2024 and not Vedal's V3 speaking-TTS project, which he said in June 2025 was still unshipped).
